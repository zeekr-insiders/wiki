## Purchase Process

### Steps to Purchase
1. **Configure Your Vehicle**
   - Choose variant
   - Select colours and options
   - Calculate final price

2. **Finance Pre-Approval**
   - Submit application
   - Provide documentation
   - Receive approval

3. **Place Order**
   - Sign purchase agreement
   - Pay deposit
   - Receive order confirmation

4. **Delivery Preparation**
   - Vehicle allocation
   - Pre-delivery inspection
   - Delivery scheduling

5. **Handover**
   - Final inspection
   - Documentation completion
   - Keys and ownership transfer
