# New South Wales Resources

EV ownership resources specific to New South Wales.

## Government Incentives

### Stamp Duty - Exemption Has Ended

!!! warning "The NSW EV stamp duty exemption ended on 31 December 2023"
    If you buy an EV in NSW today, you pay full standard stamp duty like any other car. Only buyers who purchased or placed a deposit before 1 January 2024 kept their eligibility.

The exemption (full stamp duty relief on EVs under $78,000) and the $3,000 EV rebate both closed to new purchases from 1 January 2024. There is currently no NSW-specific purchase subsidy for EVs.

What NSW buyers still benefit from:

- The federal FBT exemption on eligible EVs via novated leasing - see the [finance guide](../../buying/finance.md)
- Concessional registration rates for zero and low emission vehicles - check [Service NSW](https://www.service.nsw.gov.au) for current figures
- Ongoing state investment in charging infrastructure ($150m+ committed)

### Coming in 2027: Road User Charge and Stamp Duty Return

NSW has legislated that all EVs (including plug-in hybrids) will start paying stamp duty **and** a distance-based road user charge from **1 July 2027**, or earlier if EVs reach 30% of new vehicle sales. Worth factoring into your running-cost sums if you're buying now. Details at the [NSW Government road user charge page](https://www.nsw.gov.au/driving-boating-and-transport/nsw-governments-electric-vehicle-strategy/road-user-charge).

### Registration

NSW registration includes Green Slip (CTP insurance). Some insurers offer discounts for EVs - worth asking when you get quotes.

### Strata Considerations

Many Sydney residents live in apartments. Key considerations:

- Strata approval typically required
- Common property vs dedicated parking
- Metering options (individual or building)
- Check [NSW Fair Trading](https://www.fairtrading.nsw.gov.au) for EV charging in strata guidance
