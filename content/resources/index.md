## Quick Reference

### Emergency Contacts

| Service | Contact |
|---------|---------|
| **Zeekr Roadside Assistance** | 1800 093 357 |
| **Emergency Services** | 000 |
| **RACV (VIC)** | 13 11 11 |
| **NRMA (NSW)** | 13 11 22 |

!!! warning "EV Towing"
    Always request a **flatbed tow truck**. EVs should not be towed with wheels on the ground.

### Essential Apps

| App | Purpose |
|-----|---------|
| **Zeekr** | Vehicle control, charging status |
| **PlugShare** | Find chargers, read reviews |
| **A Better Route Planner** | Trip planning with charging |
| **Chargefox / Evie** | Network access and payment |

### Federal Incentives

| Incentive | Benefit |
|-----------|---------|
| **Fringe Benefits Tax Exemption** | Salary sacrifice EVs under $91,661 (2026-27); full exemption ends 31 March 2027 - see the [finance guide](../buying/finance.md) |
| **Luxury Car Tax** | No LCT on EVs priced under the fuel-efficient threshold |
| **Instant Asset Write-off** | Business deductions apply, capped by the car cost limit |
