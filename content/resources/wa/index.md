### Regional Considerations

!!! warning "Vast Distances"
    Western Australia's size means careful trip planning is essential. Charging infrastructure outside Perth metro is improving but still limited in many areas.

| Route | Status |
|-------|--------|
| **Perth - Margaret River** | Good coverage |
| **Perth - Geraldton** | Improving |
| **Perth - Kalgoorlie** | Limited - plan carefully |
| **Perth - Broome** | Very limited - not recommended without extensive planning |
