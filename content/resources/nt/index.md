# Northern Territory

*Last checked: 8 November 2026.*

## Incentives

The NT offers a $1,500 stamp duty subsidy for EVs until 30 June 2027 and up to five years' free registration [^1].

!!! note "Not yet confirmed"
    This comes from a single secondary source (RACV, May 2026) [^1], and other websites disagree on some state concessions. We have not checked it against the state revenue office yet. Confirm before you budget.

Check current details with the [NT Government](https://nt.gov.au).

## Service

Zeekr has no sales or service sites in the Northern Territory [^2]. The [dealers page](../../buying/dealers.md) explains who to ask about servicing.

## Driving in the Territory

Distances are long and heat cuts range. Check the chargers on each leg before you leave, carry a backup plan for every stop, pre-cool the cabin while plugged in, and check road conditions in the wet season.
