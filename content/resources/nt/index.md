### Territory Challenges

!!! warning "Remote Territory Driving"
    The NT presents unique challenges for EV owners:

    - **Vast distances** between towns
    - **Limited charging infrastructure** outside Darwin
    - **Extreme heat** can affect battery performance and range
    - **Stuart Highway** (Darwin to Adelaide) requires careful planning

### Stuart Highway

The iconic Darwin to Adelaide drive (approximately 3,000km) is challenging but increasingly possible with EVs:

| Segment | Status |
|---------|--------|
| Darwin - Katherine | Improving |
| Katherine - Tennant Creek | Limited |
| Tennant Creek - Alice Springs | Limited |
| Alice Springs - Coober Pedy | Very limited |
| Coober Pedy - Adelaide | Improving |

!!! danger "Not Yet Recommended"
    Long-distance driving through the NT in an EV requires extensive planning and backup strategies. The Zeekr 7X's long range helps, but infrastructure gaps remain significant.

## NT Climate Considerations

- **Extreme heat**: Expect reduced range in peak summer
- **Pre-conditioning**: Essential - use while plugged in
- **Parking**: Always seek shade
- **Wet season**: Some roads may be impassable
