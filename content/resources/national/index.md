# National Resources

Resources and information applicable across all of Australia for Zeekr owners.

## Charging Networks

Australia's major EV charging networks that support Zeekr vehicles:

### Ultra-Rapid Networks (150kW+)

| Network | Max Speed | Coverage | Payment |
|---------|-----------|----------|---------|
| **Chargefox** | 350kW | Nationwide | App/RFID |
| **Ampol AmpCharge** | 350kW | Major routes | App/Credit Card |
| **Evie Networks** | 350kW | Urban + Highway | App |
| **NRMA** | 350kW | NSW/ACT focused | App |
| **Tesla Supercharger** | 250kW | Opening to non-Tesla | Tesla App |

!!! tip "Zeekr 7X Charging Advantage"
    The 7X's 800V architecture means you can actually use the full speed of 350kW chargers. Most 400V competitors cap out at 150-200kW regardless of the charger's capability.

### Destination Charging (AC)

- **Chargefox**: Type 2 AC at shopping centres
- **BP Pulse**: Select BP stations
- **Hotel/Shopping**: Various Tesla Destination chargers (adaptor needed)

## Roadside Assistance

All Zeekr vehicles include roadside assistance. Key contacts:

| Situation | Contact |
|-----------|---------|
| **Zeekr Roadside** | 1800 ZEEKR AU *(confirm number)* |
| **NRMA/RACV/etc** | Your state auto club |
| **Flat Battery** | Roadside can provide mobile charge |

!!! warning "Towing EVs"
    Always request a **flatbed tow truck**. EVs should not be towed with wheels on the ground as it can damage the drive motors.

## Insurance Providers

Insurers with specific EV policies or experience:

- **RACV** (VIC)
- **NRMA** (NSW)
- **Youi** - Online EV quotes
- **Allianz** - EV specialist policies

!!! info "Agreed Value Recommended"
    Given EV depreciation can be unpredictable, consider an **agreed value** policy rather than market value.
