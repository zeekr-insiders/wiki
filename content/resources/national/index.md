# National

*Last checked: 8 November 2026.*

## Zeekr Roadside and customer support

Zeekr Roadside is on **1800 093 357**, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, anywhere in Australia [^1][^2]. The same number is the customer support line, 9 am to 6 pm AEST, Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays; email customersupportau@zeekrlife.com [^1].

When you call roadside, have your VIN, your location (nearest cross street), a description of the problem and a contact number ready [^1].

Roadside runs for 5 years from first registration [^3]. The first 1,000 Australian 7X buyers were offered 7 years [^4]. Under the April 2026 terms, cover includes [^2]:

- phone help
- a flat 12-volt battery test or jump-start (a replacement battery may be charged to you)
- a flat-tyre gel repair or a tow to the nearest tyre repairer
- help with lost or locked-in keys
- a tow to the nearest authorised Zeekr retailer
- one one-way taxi, up to $100 including GST
- recovery if you are bogged on a legally trafficable 2WD road
- if you are more than 100 km from home and the car is off the road for more than 24 hours: up to three nights' accommodation (up to $250 a night), or a hire car for up to five days (up to $500), or other transport (up to $500).

The car must have been sold by Zeekr Australia, be registered, and be roadworthy and well maintained [^2].

!!! warning "Sources differ: out of charge"
    A 2025 summary sheet lists a tow to the nearest compatible charger if you run out of charge, and a $150 taxi limit [^5]. The April 2026 terms list a $100 taxi and do not mention running out of charge [^2]. Zeekr Roadside does not offer a mobile charge in either document. If the high-voltage battery is flat, expect a tow and ask when you call.

Zeekr does not name the company that runs its roadside service [^1][^2].

## Federal tax

- **Luxury car tax:** for FY2026-27 (from 1 July 2026) the threshold for fuel-efficient vehicles, which includes EVs, is $91,661; for other vehicles it is $80,809 [^6][^7]. Every 009 grade is above it; the 7X and X are below it.
- **Fringe benefits tax:** see the [finance guide](../../buying/finance.md). A change from April 2027 has been reported but is not yet confirmed.
