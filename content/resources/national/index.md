!!! tip "Zeekr 7X Charging Advantage"
    The 7X's 800V architecture means you can actually use the full speed of 350kW chargers. Most 400V competitors cap out at 150-200kW regardless of the charger's capability.

## Government Incentives

### Federal

| Incentive | How it works |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Fringe Benefits Tax Exemption** | No FBT on eligible EVs under the fuel-efficient LCT threshold ($91,661 for 2026-27) via novated lease. Full exemption ends 31 March 2027, after which only EVs under $75,000 keep it - see the [finance guide](../../buying/finance.md) |
| **Luxury Car Tax** | EVs priced under the fuel-efficient threshold ($91,661 for 2026-27) simply attract no LCT. It's a threshold, not a rebate - EVs above it (like the 009) pay 33% LCT on the amount over |
| **Instant Asset Write-off** | Business purchases are limited by the $20,000 per-asset cap and the passenger car cost limit (~$69,674 for 2025-26) - no Zeekr can be fully written off in year one. Talk to your accountant |

## Roadside Assistance

All Zeekr vehicles include roadside assistance. Key contacts:

| Situation | Contact |
|-----------|---------|
| **Zeekr Roadside** | 1800 093 357 |
| **NRMA/RACV/etc** | Your state auto club |
| **Flat Battery** | Roadside can provide mobile charge |

!!! warning "Towing EVs"
    Always request a **flatbed tow truck**. EVs should not be towed with wheels on the ground as it can damage the drive motors.

## Insurance Providers

Insurers with specific EV policies or experience:

- **RACV** (VIC)
- **NRMA** (NSW)
- **Youi** - Online EV quotes
- **Allianz** - EV specialist policies
- **InsureMyTesla** (Zurich, via Tesla) - Tesla-only, but a sign EV-specific products are maturing here

!!! info "Agreed Value Recommended"
    Given EV depreciation can be unpredictable, consider an **agreed value** policy rather than market value.
