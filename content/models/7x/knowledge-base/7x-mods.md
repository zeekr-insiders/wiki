## Dashcam Installation

**Difficulty:** Medium
**Parts Needed:**
-   Fuse Tap (Micro2 or Mini - Check fuse box)
-   Trim Removal Tool
-   High Endurance MicroSD Card

**The "Clean" Install:**
The Zeekr 7X has a fuse box in the passenger footwell.
-   **ACC (Ignition):** Fuse #12 (Cigarette Lighter) - *Verify with multimeter.*
-   **BATT (Constant):** Fuse #18 (Door Locks) - *Verify with multimeter.*
-   **Ground:** There is a 10mm bolt behind the kick panel.

## Screen Protection

**Difficulty:** Easy
**Verdict:** Essential.
The 16-inch screen is a fingerprint magnet.
-   **Matte vs Gloss:** Go for **Matte**. It reduces glare significantly, especially with the panoramic roof.
-   **Installation Tip:** Put the screen in "Screen Cleaning Mode" (under Display Settings) so you don't ghost-touch a factory reset while wiping it down.

## Mud Flaps

**Difficulty:** Easy (No Drill)
**Verdict:** Recommended for country driving.
The 7X has wide hips. The rear tyres kick up stones directly onto the rear bumper paint.
-   **Source:** AliExpress / TaoBao usually have "No Drill" clip-on options specific to the 7X (look for "Zeekr 7X Mudguards").

## De-Badging

**Difficulty:** Easy
**Verdict:** Cleaner look.
-   **Rear "ZEEKR" Text:** It's just adhesive. Heat gun (low) + Dental Floss + Goo Gone.
-   **AWD Badge:** Same process.
