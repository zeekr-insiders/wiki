# Zeekr 7X - Known Issues & "Insider" Fixes

This page tracks common quirks, software bugs, and configuration tips for the Australian Zeekr 7X.

!!! warning "Community Contributed"
    These are user-reported issues. Always consult your dealer for warranty claims.

## Driver Monitoring System (DMS)

**The Issue:** The DMS can be overly aggressive, beeping if you look at the screen for navigation or check your mirrors for "too long".
**The Insider Fix:**
1.  Go to **Settings > Safety > Driver Assist**.
2.  Set "Driver Attention Warning" to **"Low"** or **"Standard"**. High is too sensitive for Australian highway driving.
3.  *Note:* You cannot permanently disable this (ADR requirement), but you can create a "Shortcut" in the pull-down menu to toggle it quickly if it bugs you on a specific drive.

## Speed Limit Warning (ISA)

**The Issue:** The car beeps every time the speed limit changes or if you go 1km/h over.
**The Insider Fix:**

-   **Double Tap:** You can often mute the ISA (Intelligent Speed Assist) by double-tapping a specific button on the steering wheel (Check your owner's manual for the specific 7X mapping, often the "Mute" or "Return" button).
-   **Permanent Setting:** Under **Settings > Safety**, set the warning offset to "+3km/h" or "+5km/h" if the software allows (version dependent).

## Charging Port Locking

**The Issue:** The Type 2 cable won't release after charging.
**The Insider Fix:**
1.  **Unlock the Car:** Ensure the car is unlocked. The cable locks with the doors.
2.  **Emergency Release:** Open the boot. On the side panel behind the charge port, there is usually a small pull-cord or panel to manually release the pin. *Know where this is before you need it.*

## Connectivity

**The Issue:** Spotify/Music streaming buffers.
**The Fix:**

-   Check if your 3-month complimentary data plan has expired.
-   Reboot the infotainment: Hold down the two scroll wheels on the steering wheel for 10 seconds (standard reboot procedure for Zeekr OS).
