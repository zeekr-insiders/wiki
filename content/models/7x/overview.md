# Zeekr 7X Australia - Model Overview

**The "Family-First" Electric SUV**

The Zeekr 7X is the brand's first mainstream family SUV, aiming directly at the Tesla Model Y. It brings 800V architecture (super fast charging) and a luxury interior to the mid-size SUV segment.

!!! success "Quick Verdict"
    If you want faster charging than a Tesla and a more luxurious interior than a BYD, the 7X is the sweet spot. Just be aware of the larger footprint on narrow Melbourne streets.

## Australian Launch Details

-   **Status:** Deliveries commenced October 15, 2025.
-   **Warranty:** 7 Years / Unlimited KM (First 1000 orders), Standard 5 Years.

## Insider "Need to Know"

### 1. The "Golden Battery" (LFP) vs NMC
The base RWD model uses Zeekr's in-house "Golden Battery" (LFP). It charges ridiculously fast (10-80% in ~13 mins) and is safer/longer-lasting than NMC.
*   **Insider Tip:** Don't be afraid of the "smaller" 75kWh battery. The charging speed makes up for the range difference on road trips, and you can charge it to 100% daily without degradation anxiety.

### 2. 800V Architecture
This is the game changer. Most competitors (BYD Atto 3, Tesla Model Y) are 400V. The 7X is 800V.
*   **What this means:** If you pull up to a 350kW Chargefox ultra-rapid charger, you will *actually* get 300kW+ speeds. You'll be done charging before you finish your coffee.

### 3. V2L is Standard
All Australian models come with Vehicle-to-Load (V2L). You can power a campsite, coffee machine, or charge another EV at 3.3kW.
*   **Check on Delivery:** Ensure the V2L discharge adapter is in the boot. It plugs into the external charging port.

## Key Features (Australian Spec)

-   **Infotainment:** Qualcomm 8295 chip (snappy, no lag).
-   **Screen:** 16-inch 3.5K Mini-LED (stunning resolution).
-   **Roof:** Fixed panoramic glass with **electric sunshade** (Crucial for Australian summers!).
-   **Towing:** 2,000kg braked towing capacity (check towball down weight in manual).
