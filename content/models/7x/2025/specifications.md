## Standard Equipment Breakdown

### RWD (Base)
*   **Audio:** Zeekr Sound (10 Speakers).
*   **Display:** 16" Center Screen + Driver Cluster.
*   **Seats:** 14-way Power Driver / 10-way Passenger (Heated).
*   **Roof:** Panoramic with Electric Sunshade.
*   **Tech:** 360 Camera, V2L (3.3kW), Sentry Mode.
*   **Wheels:** 19-inch Alloy.

### Long Range RWD (Adds over RWD)
*   **Audio:** **Zeekr Sound Pro (21 Speakers, 7.1.4 Dolby Atmos).**
*   **HUD:** **36-inch AR Heads-Up Display.**
*   **Seats:** Ventilated & Massaging (Front).
*   **Glass:** Privacy Glass (Rear).
*   **Steering:** Power Adjustable Column.
*   **Wheels:** 20-inch Alloy.

### AWD Performance (Adds over Long Range)
*   **Suspension:** **Air Suspension + CDC (Continuous Damping).**
*   **Doors:** Power Operated Doors (Auto Open/Close).
*   **Seats:** Nappa Leather Upholstery.
*   **Comfort:** Electric Rear Side Sunshades.
*   **Wheels:** 21-inch Forged Alloy.

!!! info "Tow Bar Note"
    The tow bar is an optional factory-fit or dealer-fit accessory. Ensure the "Tow Mode" software is active if fitting aftermarket.
