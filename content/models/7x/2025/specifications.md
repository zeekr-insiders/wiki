## Powertrain & Performance

### RWD / Long Range RWD
-   **Motor:** Single Rear Permanent Magnet Synchronous
-   **Power:** 310 kW
-   **Torque:** 440 Nm
-   **0-100 km/h:** 6.0 seconds (claimed)

### AWD Performance
-   **Motors:** Dual (Front + Rear)
-   **Power:** 475 kW Combined (165kW Front + 310kW Rear)
-   **Torque:** 710 Nm Combined
-   **0-100 km/h:** 3.8 seconds (claimed)
-   **Suspension:** Air Suspension + CDC (Continuous Damping Control)

## Battery & Charging

### Standard Range (RWD)
-   **Chemistry:** LFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate) - "Golden Battery"
-   **Capacity:** 75 kWh (Usable)
-   **Voltage:** 800V Class
-   **Max DC Charge:** 450 kW (Peak)
-   **10-80% Charge:** ~13 minutes

### Long Range (RWD & AWD)
-   **Chemistry:** NMC (Nickel Manganese Cobalt)
-   **Capacity:** 100 kWh (Usable)
-   **Voltage:** 800V Class
-   **Max DC Charge:** 420 kW (Peak)
-   **10-80% Charge:** ~16 minutes
