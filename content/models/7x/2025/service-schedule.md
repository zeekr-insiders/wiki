# Zeekr 7X servicing and warranty

*Last checked: 15 November 2026.*

!!! info "2027 7X (MY27)"
    The updated 2027 7X (MY27) delivers from December 2026. Figures on this page are for the MY25/MY26 car unless marked. See [Which 7X do I have?](../which-7x.md).

## Service interval

Zeekr Australia's aftersales notice for the 7X, from its Head of Aftersales, sets the manufacturer service at **every 2 years or 40,000 km**, whichever comes first, with **optional health checks every year or 20,000 km** [^1][^2]. The Driven published the notice's wording on 22 September 2025 [^1], and an owner has posted the notice itself on Whirlpool [^2]. Zeekr's owner's manuals for Singapore and the Netherlands give the same 2-year interval for the 7X [^3][^4]. Zeekr's own service page doesn't list an interval [^5].

You'll still see **12 months or 20,000 km** quoted, for example by RACV and Carsales [^6][^7]. That figure describes the yearly capped-price plan, which counts the optional health checks: RACV's five-year total is "based on 12-month/20,000km intervals" [^6].

Your Service and Warranty Booklet is the authority. Zeekr's warranty policy points to it for the schedule and doesn't mention the optional checks [^8]. If you plan to skip them, ask your dealer to confirm in writing that it won't affect your warranty.

## What each service includes

Every visit includes the required vehicle checks and any software updates not delivered over the air. On top of that, the notice lists these parts for the MY25/MY26 car [^2][^driven]:

| Service | Type | Replaced | Price |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 12 months / 20,000 km | Optional health check | Cabin (A/C) filter | Free (see below) |
| 24 months / 40,000 km | Manufacturer service | Cabin filter, brake fluid, reduction gear oil | $605 |
| 36 months / 60,000 km | Optional health check | Cabin filter | $330 |
| 48 months / 80,000 km | Manufacturer service | Cabin filter, brake fluid, reduction gear oil, coolant | $1,150 |

Coolant is the long-interval item: it's first replaced at the 48-month service [^2]. The Netherlands manual gives it as every 48 months or 80,000 km [^4].

The prices are Zeekr's recommended prices from the notice [^2]. RACV reports that Zeekr Australia couldn't confirm whether prices have risen since [^6], so ask for the current price when you book. CarExpert quotes $4,770 over 8 years or 160,000 km for the Long Range RWD [^9].

!!! note "Not yet confirmed: the year-5 price"
    Sources differ on the optional check at 5 years or 100,000 km. The notice image and CarExpert's review give $330 [^2][^10]; The Driven gives $300 [^1]. The widely quoted five-year total of $2,385 only adds up with $300 [^6][^11]. Your dealer can confirm.

Tyre rotation, wiper blades, an air-conditioning regas and the key fob battery are not scheduled items in any of Zeekr's published schedules [^2][^3][^4]. An owner reports their dealer rotating the tyres at the first service [^2], so ask for it when you book. Anything else the dealer recommends, such as new tyres or wipers, is quoted and charged separately [^2].

## Free servicing offers

### MY25/MY26: first health check free

Zeekr's notice says "the first year / 20,000km service will be offered free of charge to the customers", to bring owners back to the dealer for a check-over [^2][^1]. That is the 12-month optional health check in the table above.

!!! note "Not yet confirmed: who qualifies"
    The notice is the only statement of this benefit we've found: there are no published terms, cut-off date or grace period [^2]. One owner reports their dealer hadn't heard of it until Zeekr's head office confirmed it [^2]. Book through the dealer and ask them to check with Zeekr Australia if they query it.

The offer to the first 1,000 Australian buyers did not include servicing. It covered a 7-year warranty, 7 years of roadside assistance, a home charger, charging cables and free colour choice [^12][^13].

### MY27 launch offer

Zeekr's terms for the MY27 launch offer are on its offers page [^offers]:

- **Who:** buyers who purchase, or order with a deposit, a new MY27 7X from an authorised Australian dealer between 1 November and 31 December 2026. Retail buyers only, including ABN holders; fleet, government and rental buyers are excluded unless Zeekr approves, as are demonstrators.
- **What:** the first two scheduled services free, the 12 months / 20,000 km and 24 months / 40,000 km services.
- **When:** within 2 years of delivery, with a grace period of 60 days or 1,500 km past each service. Unused services then lapse.
- **Not covered:** wear items such as tyres, brake pads and wiper blades, repairs, accident damage and unscheduled work.
- **Where:** an authorised Zeekr service centre, or a provider Zeekr nominates. One entitlement per car, non-transferable and with no cash value.

The offer was first capped at 2,000 orders. Zeekr dropped the cap in early November 2026, so it now runs on dates alone [^14][^15].

The terms call both visits scheduled services [^16]. Whether the MY27 car moves to a yearly interval is not yet confirmed: check your MY27 booklet.

## Warranty

These are Zeekr's standard terms for private-use cars [^warranty]:

| Cover | Term |
| :--- | :--- |
| Vehicle warranty | 5 years, unlimited km |
| High-voltage battery | 8 years or 160,000 km; repair or replacement if state of health falls below 70% |
| Corrosion (perforation) and paint | 5 years |
| Roadside assistance | 5 years |
| 12-volt battery and key fob battery | 12 months or 20,000 km |
| Accessories fitted after delivery | 24 months or 20,000 km |

The first 1,000 Australian 7X buyers were offered a 7-year, unlimited-km warranty and 7 years of roadside assistance [^12]. If you bought under an offer, your contract sets your terms.

## Keeping your warranty

Zeekr's warranty policy does not say you must service at a Zeekr dealer. What it does say [^warranty]:

- The car must be serviced and maintained to the Zeekr schedule in the Service and Warranty Booklet, using parts that meet Zeekr's specifications.
- Keep detailed records of each service: date, odometer, work done, parts and fluids, and the job card. You need to show the booklet when you claim.
- Faults caused by servicing that was not done to Zeekr's specifications, or not done by a "duly certified and qualified" workshop, are excluded.
- Warranty claims are made through an authorised Zeekr dealer. Report a fault within 7 calendar days, or as soon as practicable.
- Faults caused by unapproved modifications, including electrical modifications, are excluded.

The ACCC's position is that a manufacturer's warranty typically lets you choose your own repairer for servicing, as long as the manufacturer's servicing standards are met; some warranties require repairs claimed under warranty to be done in the dealer network [^17]. Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law apply in addition to the warranty, and Zeekr's policy says it does not limit them [^8].

In practice: keep to the schedule, keep every record, use a qualified workshop and parts that meet Zeekr's specifications, wherever you service. Free MY27 launch-offer servicing must be done at an authorised Zeekr service centre or a provider Zeekr nominates [^16].

## Roadside assistance

Zeekr Roadside is on **1800 093 357**, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, anywhere in Australia [^5][^18]. Have your VIN, your location (nearest cross street), a description of the problem and a contact number ready.

Cover includes phone help, a flat 12-volt battery test or jump-start, a flat-tyre repair or tow to a tyre repairer, help with lost or locked-in keys, and a tow to the nearest authorised Zeekr retailer [^18]. If the high-voltage battery is flat, a tow is the likely outcome: ask when you call.
