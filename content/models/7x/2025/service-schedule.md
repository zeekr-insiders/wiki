# 2025 Zeekr 7X Service Schedule & Costs

**The "Low Maintenance" Advantage**

One of the biggest "Insider" perks of the Zeekr 7X is the service interval. Unlike some competitors (who want you back every 12 months), Zeekr officially requires servicing only **every 2 years or 40,000km**.

## Insider Tips

### 1. The "Optional" 12-Month Check
Dealers may encourage you to come in every year.
*   **The Truth:** The 12-month/20,000km check is *optional* according to Zeekr Australia's maintenance schedule.
*   **The Exception:** If you drive in "severe conditions" (dusty roads, frequent towing), you *should* get it checked annually.
*   **First One Free:** Many dealers offer the first 12-month check for free. Take it if it's free, but confirm before handing over the keys.

### 2. What happens at the 4-Year Service?
The jump in price to ~$1,150 at 4 years is usually due to:
*   Brake fluid flush.
*   Coolant checks/flush (battery thermal management systems are complex).
*   Cabin air filter replacement (HEPA filter).

### 3. Tyre Wear
**Warning:** The 7X is heavy (2.3+ tonnes) and powerful.
*   **RWD models:** Expect rear tyres to wear faster. Rotate them if possible (unless staggered fitment - check your specific rim setup).
*   **AWD models:** Expect more even wear, but aggressive driving will chew through the factory Michelins in <30,000km.

## Warranty Terms

*   **Vehicle:** 5 Years / Unlimited km (Private Use).
*   **Battery:** 8 Years / 160,000 km (guaranteed to >70% capacity).
*   **Roadside Assist:** 5 Years complimentary.
