## Insider Tips

### 1. The 2-Year Interval is Real
Unlike some competitors who push annual services, Zeekr genuinely only requires servicing every 2 years or 40,000km.

**The exception:** If you drive in "severe conditions" (dusty roads, frequent towing, extreme climates), you might want interim checks. But these aren't mandatory for warranty.

### 2. Book Ahead
With the 7X being popular and service centres still ramping up, book your service 2-4 weeks in advance. Don't wait until you're overdue.

### 3. Tyre Wear
**Warning:** The 7X is heavy (2.3+ tonnes) and powerful.

- **RWD models:** Expect rear tyres to wear faster. Rotate them regularly (every 10,000km) if your fitment allows.
- **AWD models:** More even wear, but aggressive driving will chew through the factory tyres in under 30,000km.
- **Budget:** Quality replacement tyres are $300-400 each. Plan for a full set every 40,000-60,000km depending on driving style.

## Maintaining Your Warranty

To keep your warranty valid:

- **Service on schedule:** Don't miss your 2-year/40,000km services
- **Use authorised dealers:** Services must be done at Zeekr dealerships
- **Keep records:** Save all service receipts and documentation
- **Report issues promptly:** Don't delay on warranty claims

## Common Maintenance Items (Out of Warranty)

Things you'll pay for separately:

**Tyres:** Not covered by warranty. Expect to replace every 40,000-60,000km depending on driving style. Budget $1,200-1,600 for a full set of quality tyres.

**Wiper blades:** Replaced as needed. ~$50-80 per set.

**Brake pads:** EVs use regenerative braking, so pads last much longer (60,000-100,000km typical). First replacement usually $400-600.

**12V battery:** The small battery that runs accessories. Usually lasts 4-6 years. Replacement ~$200-300.

**Cabin air filter:** Replaced during services if needed. ~$80-150 for HEPA filter.

## Service Centre Tips

**What to bring:**

- Driver's licence
- Service booking confirmation
- Any specific concerns written down
- List of warning lights or issues

**What to expect:**

- Service takes 2-4 hours typically
- Many dealers offer courtesy vehicles (book ahead)
- Some offer pick-up/drop-off service
- You'll get a written report of work done

**Pro tip:** If you have multiple small issues (squeaks, rattles, minor trim issues), list them all when booking. Easier to fix everything in one visit.
