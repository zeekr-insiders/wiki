## Insider Buying Tips
1.  **The "Convenience Pack" is worth it:** If buying the Long Range RWD, the extra $4k for the HUD and Sound System drastically changes the cabin feel.
2.  **White Interiors:** Zeekr's white interior is *very* white. If you wear blue jeans often, invest in a ceramic leather coating immediately.
