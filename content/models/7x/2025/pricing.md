!!! tip "ZLEV Road User Charge"
    The Victorian ZLEV Road User Charge (2.8c/km) was ruled invalid by the High Court on 18 October 2023 (*Vanderstock v Victoria*). No state currently charges one, but NSW has legislated a road user charge from 1 July 2027 and a national scheme is under federal consideration - keep an eye on it.

## Insider Buying Tips

1. **The "Convenience Pack" is worth it:** If buying the base RWD, the extra $4k for the HUD and Sound System drastically changes the cabin feel. (The Long Range and AWD already include it.)
2. **White Interiors:** Zeekr's white interior is *very* white. If you wear blue jeans often, invest in a ceramic leather coating immediately.
