# Which 7X do I have?

*Last checked: 8 November 2026.*

Two versions of the 7X are sold in Australia:

- **MY25/MY26 7X**: the launch car. Cars may be badged MY25 or MY26; we have not found a published difference between them, so this wiki treats them as one car.
- **MY27 7X (the 2027 7X)**: the updated car. Orders opened on 1 November 2026 and first deliveries are expected in December 2026 [^1][^2]. It is a mid-cycle update, not a new generation: same three grades, same prices, same batteries [^1].

## Quick ways to tell them apart

| Look at | MY25/MY26 | MY27 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Rear number plate | On the rear bumper | On the tailgate [^1] |
| Front of the car | Original air intakes and grille shutter | Revised air intakes, narrower active grille shutter [^1] |
| Rear diffuser | Original | Redesigned [^1] |
| Centre console | Original layout | Reworked, cupholders moved, extra storage [^1] |
| Built-in dashcam (DVR) | Not listed in any source we found | Standard on all grades [^1][^2] |
| Rear privacy glass | Check your grade | Standard on all grades [^2][^3] |
| Tech Grey with black roof paint | Not in the launch colour list [^6] | Offered [^1][^4] |

The number plate is the easiest tell from a car park: on the bumper means MY25/MY26, on the tailgate means MY27.

!!! note "Not yet confirmed"
    We have not found a published build-date range, compliance plate cut-off or VIN cut-off between the two versions, or any way the Zeekr app shows which one you have. If you need to be certain, ask your dealer to check the VIN.

## What changed

| Item | MY25/MY26 | MY27 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Price (RRP before on-road costs) | $57,900 / $63,900 / $72,900 | Unchanged [^1][^2] |
| RWD and Long Range power | 310 kW / 440 Nm | 335 kW / 520 Nm [^1] |
| Performance AWD power | 475 kW / 710 Nm | 500 kW / 790 Nm [^1] |
| 0-100 km/h (RWD / Long Range / AWD) | 6.0 / 6.0 / 3.8 s [^4] | 5.6 / 5.5 / 3.7 s [^1] |
| WLTP range (RWD / Long Range / AWD) | 480 / 615 / 543 km [^4] | 482 / 627 / 544 km [^1] |
| Battery | 75 kWh LFP (RWD), 100 kWh NMC (Long Range, AWD) | Unchanged [^1] |
| Peak DC charging | 450 kW (RWD), 420 kW (Long Range, AWD) | Unchanged, 800V system [^1] |
| AC charging | 22 kW | Unchanged [^1] |

!!! warning "Check which table you are reading"
    Zeekr's own 7X page carries two comparison tables, one with MY25/MY26 figures and one with MY27 figures [^4]. If you copy a number from it, check which car it describes.

## Still unknown for the MY27

- Towing capacity (the MY25/MY26 car is rated 2,000 kg braked [^5]).
- Boot volume (539 L is the MY25/MY26 figure [^6]).
- The service interval and capped-price schedule.
- Whether MY25/MY26 cars will get the DVR or other MY27 features by over-the-air update. Nothing has been published.
