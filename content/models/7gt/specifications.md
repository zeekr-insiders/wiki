# Zeekr 7GT specifications

*Last checked: 8 November 2026.*

!!! warning "Not yet on sale in Australia"
    First deliveries are expected in the first quarter of 2027 [^1][^2]. The figures here are those Zeekr gave for the Australian line-up [^2][^3][^4]; Zeekr Australia says local specifications and features may differ from the overseas car [^1], so check them against the final spec sheet before you order.

## Australian line-up

The 7GT comes in three grades [^2][^3][^4]:

| Spec | RWD | Long Range RWD | Performance AWD |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Battery | 75 kWh LFP | 100 kWh NCM | 100 kWh NCM |
| Power / torque | 335 kW / 520 Nm | 335 kW / 520 Nm | 500 kW / 790 Nm |
| 0-100 km/h | 5.2 s | 5.0 s | 3.1 s |
| WLTP range | Up to 525 km | Up to 670 km | Up to 590 km |
| DC 10-80% | 11 min | 15 min | 15 min |
| Wheels | 19-inch | 19-inch | 20-inch forged |

The Performance AWD adds active air suspension and continuously variable dampers [^4][^5]. Zeekr's "10% to 80% in as little as 15 minutes" applies to the Long Range RWD and Performance AWD [^1].

Earlier reports from August 2026 carried slower times (0-100 km/h in 6.5, 5.5 and 3.5 s) and about 550 km for the AWD [^6]; the figures above are the September 2026 announcement and match the outputs of the updated 2027 7X [^7]. The European-market AWD is rated at 475 kW / 710 Nm and 558 km [^8][^9].

## Charging

- **Architecture:** 800V [^4][^5].
- **Peak DC rate:** up to 420 kW on the Long Range and Performance AWD [^1][^2][^4]. Some reports give 450 kW [^8], and 480 kW for the European car [^9]; Zeekr Australia has not confirmed a final figure.
- **10-80%:** 11 minutes (RWD) and 15 minutes (Long Range, AWD) [^2][^3]. Your time depends on the charger, the battery temperature and the starting charge.
- **AC rate:** not yet published for the Australian car.

## Size and practicality

| Spec | Figure |
| :--- | :--- |
| Body | Five-door shooting brake, five seats [^4][^5] |
| Length / width / height / wheelbase | 4,817 / 1,910 / 1,456 / 2,900 mm [^4] |
| Boot (seats up / folded) | 456 / 1,390 L [^2][^4] |
| Underbonnet storage | 65 L (European car) [^9] |
| Towing | - |
| Kerb weight | - |

Zeekr has not published towing or kerb weight figures for the Australian car.

The China-market 7GT is a different size: 4,864 x 1,900 x 1,460 mm, a 2,925 mm wheelbase and a 645 L boot [^10]. Don't mix its figures with the Australian ones. Boot and rear-seat space trail the 7X, which has a 539 L boot [^8].

## Safety and driver assistance

- **Crash rating:** five stars from Euro NCAP. ANCAP has not yet rated the 7GT [^4][^8].
- **Driver assistance:** the Zeekr Assisted Drive Level 2 suite [^5], with adaptive cruise, blind-spot monitoring, lane keep, traffic sign recognition and surround view [^4]. Check the final equipment list when you order.

## Technology

The overseas car has a 15.4-inch touchscreen, 13-inch driver display, 35.5-inch AR head-up display, Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, two wireless phone chargers, and 14-way power front seats that are heated, ventilated and massaging [^4][^5]. Australian seat upholstery has not been announced [^4].

## Platform and the 7X

The 7GT and 7X share Zeekr's 800V architecture [^5][^8]. You will see it called SEA, SEA2 or PMA2+ [^5][^8][^11]. The 7GT's Australian outputs and battery sizes match the 2027 7X [^7].

## Range ratings and warranty

- **WLTP, not CLTC:** the range figures above are WLTP. The China-market 650 km and 825 km claims use a different test and are not comparable [^10].
- **Warranty:** expected to be 5 years with unlimited km, and 8 years or 160,000 km on the battery [^3][^5].
