# Zeekr 7GT specifications

*Last checked: 8 November 2026.*

!!! warning "Not yet on sale in Australia"
    The 7GT is confirmed for Australia with first deliveries expected in Q1 2027 [^1][^2]. Zeekr Australia has not published specifications on its 7GT page and says Australian specifications and features may differ from the overseas car shown [^1]. The figures below are what Australian outlets have reported, plus clearly labelled overseas figures. Treat all of them as provisional until Zeekr Australia or the ADR compliance listing confirms them.

## Australian line-up as reported

Source: Australian press reports of Zeekr's local line-up [^2][^3][^4]. Not yet on Zeekr Australia's own page.

| | RWD | Long Range RWD | Performance AWD |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Battery | 75 kWh LFP | 100 kWh NCM | 100 kWh NCM |
| Power / torque | 335 kW / 520 Nm | 335 kW / 520 Nm | 500 kW / 790 Nm |
| 0-100 km/h | 5.2 s | 5.0 s | 3.1 s |
| WLTP range | Up to 525 km | Up to 670 km | Up to 590 km |
| DC 10-80% | 11 min | 15 min | 15 min |
| Wheels | 19-inch | 19-inch | 20-inch forged |

Performance AWD also gets active air suspension and continuously variable dampers [^4][^5]. Zeekr's page names the Long Range RWD and Performance AWD for its "10% to 80% in as little as 15 minutes" claim [^1].

!!! note "Sources differ"
    Earlier reports in August 2026 quoted different numbers for the same grades: 0-100 km/h of 6.5, 5.5 and 3.5 s, and about 550 km for the AWD [^6]. A European-market preview gave 475 kW / 710 Nm and 558 km for the AWD [^7][^8]. The September 2026 figures above are the most recent and match the updated 2027 7X outputs [^9].

## Charging

- Architecture: 800V [^4][^5].
- Peak DC rate: Zeekr's page says "a 420kW charger" [^1], and The Driven and CarExpert quote up to 420 kW for the Long Range and AWD [^2][^4]. NRMA says up to 450 kW [^7], and a European-market report says up to 480 kW [^8]. **Australian peak rate is not yet confirmed**; 420 kW is the figure closest to Zeekr's own page.
- 10-80% time: 11 minutes (RWD), 15 minutes (Long Range, AWD) as reported [^2][^3]. What you see depends on the charger, battery temperature and state of charge.
- AC rate: Not yet confirmed for Australia.

| Underbonnet storage | 65 L | European-market report [^8]; Australian figure not yet confirmed |
| Towing | Not yet confirmed for Australia | |

The China-market car is listed at 4,864 x 1,900 x 1,460 mm with a 2,925 mm wheelbase and a 645 L boot [^10]. That is a different car from the one reported for Australia, so do not mix the two sets of figures. The NRMA reviewer found boot and rear space trail the 7X (539 L boot) [^7].

## Safety and driver assistance

- Euro NCAP: five stars (European rating). **ANCAP rating: not yet announced** [^4][^7].
- Driver assistance reported for Australia: Zeekr Assisted Drive Level 2 suite [^5]; adaptive cruise, blind-spot monitoring, lane keep, traffic sign recognition and surround view [^4]. Final Australian equipment is not yet confirmed.

## Technology (overseas car, may differ in Australia)

15.4-inch touchscreen, 13-inch driver display, 35.5-inch AR head-up display, Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, two wireless phone chargers, 14-way power front seats that are heated, ventilated and massaging [^4][^5]. Seat upholstery for Australia is unconfirmed [^4].

## Platform and the 7X

Both cars are 800V electric cars from Zeekr [^5][^7]. Outlets name the shared platform differently: EV Central says "Sustainable Experience Architecture (SEA)" [^5], NRMA says 800V "SEA2" [^7], and Zecar says both cars use "PMA2+" [^11]. **The platform name is conflicting**; the wiki should say "shared 800V architecture" until Zeekr states it. Compared with the 7X, the 7GT's reported Australian outputs and batteries match the 2027 7X [^9].

## Not in Australian sources

- WLTP versus CLTC: the figures above are WLTP. The China-market car's 650 km and 825 km claims are China-market and not comparable [^10].
- Warranty: reported as 5 years unlimited km, 8 years / 160,000 km battery [^3][^5], but not on Zeekr's 7GT page [^1].
