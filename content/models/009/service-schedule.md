# Zeekr 009 servicing and warranty

*Last checked: 15 November 2026.*

## Service interval

The 009 is serviced every **12 months or 20,000 km**, whichever comes first [^1][^2][^3]. It launched in 2025 on 2 years or 40,000 km [^4], so if you have an early car, check your Service and Warranty Booklet, which is the authority [^5].

## What each service includes

Zeekr Australia doesn't publish the 009's service list online. Zeekr's schedules for Malaysia and Singapore, on the same 12-month or 20,000 km interval, follow this pattern [^6][^7]:

| Service | Replaced |
| :--- | :--- |
| Every service | Cabin (A/C) filter, plus a maintenance inspection |
| Every 2 years or 40,000 km | Brake fluid and reduction gear oil (front on FWD, front and rear on AWD) |
| Every 4 years or 80,000 km | Coolant |

They are a guide to what your dealer will do, not an Australian promise. The Singapore manual halves the brake fluid and cabin filter intervals in harsh conditions such as dust, hilly or humid areas and extreme temperatures [^7].

Neither schedule lists a separate air suspension item, tyre rotation, wiper blades, an air-conditioning regas or a key fob battery [^6][^7]. If you want the air suspension or a tyre rotation looked at, ask when you book.

## Capped-price servicing

Capped-price servicing covers 8 years, at **$4,920 in total**, an average of $615 a year [^1][^2]. Ask your dealer for the price of each service when you book.

Zeekr's offers page lists no current 009 offer [^8], and the 009 run-out deals of July 2026 didn't include servicing [^9][^10].

## Warranty

These are Zeekr's standard terms for private-use cars [^5]:

| Cover | Term |
| :--- | :--- |
| Vehicle warranty | 5 years, unlimited km |
| High-voltage battery | 8 years or 160,000 km; repair or replacement if state of health falls below 70% |
| Corrosion (perforation) and paint | 5 years |
| Roadside assistance | 5 years |
| 12-volt battery and key fob battery | 12 months or 20,000 km |
| Accessories fitted after delivery | 24 months or 20,000 km |

Commercial use is covered differently; see [009 prices](pricing.md#commercial-use).

## Keeping your warranty

Zeekr's warranty policy does not say you must service at a Zeekr dealer. What it does say [^5]:

- The car must be serviced and maintained to the Zeekr schedule in the Service and Warranty Booklet, using parts that meet Zeekr's specifications.
- Keep detailed records of each service: date, odometer, work done, parts and fluids, and the job card. You need to show the booklet when you claim.
- Faults caused by servicing that was not done to Zeekr's specifications, or not done by a "duly certified and qualified" workshop, are excluded.
- Warranty claims are made through an authorised Zeekr dealer. Report a fault within 7 calendar days, or as soon as practicable.

The ACCC's position is that a manufacturer's warranty typically lets you choose your own repairer for servicing, as long as the manufacturer's servicing standards are met; some warranties require repairs claimed under warranty to be done in the dealer network [^11]. Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law apply in addition to the warranty, and Zeekr's policy says it does not limit them [^5].

## Roadside assistance

Zeekr Roadside is on **1800 093 357**, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, anywhere in Australia [^12][^13]. Cover includes phone help, a flat 12-volt battery test or jump-start, a flat-tyre repair or tow to a tyre repairer, help with lost or locked-in keys, and a tow to the nearest authorised Zeekr retailer [^13]. If the high-voltage battery is flat, a tow is the likely outcome: ask when you call.

Service locations are on the [dealers page](../../buying/dealers.md).
