## What's Included in a Service?

A typical 009 major service includes all standard Zeekr service items plus additional checks specific to the 009:

**Standard Items:**

- Multi-point vehicle inspection
- Brake system check (pads, fluid, rotors)
- Suspension and steering inspection
- Tyre condition and pressure check
- Battery health diagnostic (116 kWh system)
- Thermal management system check
- Cabin filter inspection/replacement
- Software updates if available
- Fluid level checks and top-ups

**009-Specific Checks:**

- Air suspension system inspection and calibration
- Power sliding door mechanism and lubrication
- Executive seating system inspection (massage, ventilation, recline functions)
- Rear entertainment system check
- Additional battery thermal management checks (larger 116 kWh battery)

## 009-Specific Maintenance

### Air Suspension System
The 009's dual-chamber air suspension requires periodic inspection:

- Air compressor check
- Suspension calibration
- Air spring condition assessment

### Power Sliding Doors
The electric sliding doors should be:

- Lubricated annually
- Track cleaned of debris
- Sensor alignment checked

### Large Battery Thermal Management
The 116 kWh battery has an extensive cooling system:

- Coolant levels checked at each service
- Thermal management system inspection
- Full coolant flush typically at 4-year service

## Maintaining Your Warranty

To keep your warranty valid:

- **Service on schedule:** Don't miss your 2-year/40,000km services
- **Use authorised dealers:** Services must be done at Zeekr dealerships
- **Keep records:** Save all service receipts and documentation
- **Report issues promptly:** Don't delay on warranty claims, especially for complex systems like air suspension or power doors

## Insider Tips

### 1. Tyre Costs
!!! warning "Budget Accordingly"
    The 009's size and weight (nearly 3 tonnes) combined with large tyres (typically 255/50 R20 or larger) means tyre replacement costs are significant. Budget $400-600+ per tyre for quality replacements. A full set every 40,000-50,000km could cost $2,000-2,500.

### 2. Air Suspension Maintenance
The dual-chamber air suspension is sophisticated but needs care:

- **Avoid harsh impacts:** Potholes, speed bumps at speed can damage air springs
- **Listen for leaks:** Hissing sounds or sagging overnight indicate issues
- **Regular calibration:** Should be checked during services
- **Warranty coverage:** Air suspension repairs can be expensive - maintain warranty validity

### 3. Wheel Alignment
The air suspension makes wheel alignment critical. Get it checked:

- After any kerb strike or pothole impact
- When tyres are replaced
- If you notice uneven wear or pulling to one side
- Cost: ~$150-250, but worth it to prevent $2,000+ tyre replacements

### 4. Power Sliding Door Care
Keep the electric sliding doors running smoothly:

- Keep tracks clean (vacuum out debris regularly)
- Don't force them manually when power is off
- Report any unusual noises during warranty period
- Lubrication during services keeps them quiet

### 5. Executive Service
Given the 009's premium positioning, many dealers offer VIP service packages:

- **Courtesy vehicle:** Often a premium vehicle (another Zeekr or equivalent)
- **Pick-up and delivery:** Concierge service to your home/office
- **Interior detailing:** Light clean and vacuum during service
- **Priority booking:** Flexible scheduling for busy executives

Ask your dealer what's included in their 009 service package.

## Common Maintenance Costs (Out of Warranty)

**Tyres:** $400-600 each, $2,000-2,500 for a full set

**Brake pads:** Regenerative braking extends life, but when needed: $600-900 (heavier vehicle = bigger brakes)

**12V battery:** ~$200-300 (same as other Zeekrs)

**Cabin air filter:** ~$100-150 (larger cabin = larger filter)

**Air suspension components:** $$$$ (maintain warranty coverage!)

## Service Centre Tips

**What to bring:**

- Driver's licence
- Service booking confirmation
- Detailed list of any concerns (rattles, noises, system issues)
- Note any executive seating or entertainment system issues

**What to expect:**

- Service takes 3-5 hours (more complex than 7X/X)
- Courtesy vehicle typically provided
- Many dealers offer pick-up/delivery for 009 owners
- Detailed written report of work done

**Pro tip:** Given the 009's complexity, book services 4-6 weeks in advance. Service centres need time to have the right parts available for a vehicle of this calibre.
