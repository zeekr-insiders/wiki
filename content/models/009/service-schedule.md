# Zeekr 009 servicing and warranty

*Last checked: 8 November 2026.*

## Service interval

!!! note "Not yet confirmed"
    Zeekr's suggested schedule for the 009 is reported as **12 months or 20,000 km** [^1]. That comes from a single listing and we have found no capped-price figures. Confirm in your Service and Warranty Booklet, which is the authority [^2].

## Warranty

These are Zeekr's standard terms for private-use cars [^2]:

| Cover | Term |
| :--- | :--- |
| Vehicle warranty | 5 years, unlimited km |
| High-voltage battery | 8 years or 160,000 km; repair or replacement if state of health falls below 70% |
| Corrosion (perforation) and paint | 5 years |
| Roadside assistance | 5 years |
| 12-volt battery and key fob battery | 12 months or 20,000 km |
| Accessories fitted after delivery | 24 months or 20,000 km |

Commercial use is covered differently; see [009 prices](pricing.md#commercial-use).

## Keeping your warranty

Zeekr's warranty policy does not say you must service at a Zeekr dealer. What it does say [^2]:

- The car must be serviced and maintained to the Zeekr schedule in the Service and Warranty Booklet, using parts that meet Zeekr's specifications.
- Keep detailed records of each service: date, odometer, work done, parts and fluids, and the job card. You need to show the booklet when you claim.
- Faults caused by servicing that was not done to Zeekr's specifications, or not done by a "duly certified and qualified" workshop, are excluded.
- Warranty claims are made through an authorised Zeekr dealer. Report a fault within 7 calendar days, or as soon as practicable.

The ACCC's position is that a manufacturer's warranty typically lets you choose your own repairer for servicing, as long as the manufacturer's servicing standards are met; some warranties require repairs claimed under warranty to be done in the dealer network [^3]. Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law apply in addition to the warranty, and Zeekr's policy says it does not limit them [^2].

## Roadside assistance

Zeekr Roadside is on **1800 093 357**, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, anywhere in Australia [^4][^5]. Cover includes phone help, a flat 12-volt battery test or jump-start, a flat-tyre repair or tow to a tyre repairer, help with lost or locked-in keys, and a tow to the nearest authorised Zeekr retailer [^5]. If the high-voltage battery is flat, a tow is the likely outcome: ask when you call.

Service locations are on the [dealers page](../../buying/dealers.md).
