# Zeekr 009 prices

*Last checked: 8 November 2026.*

## Recommended retail prices

Launch pricing for the 2026 009, announced in November 2025, before on-road costs [1][2]. We found no later price change.

| Grade | RRP |
| :--- | :--- |
| 009 7-seat FWD | $115,900 |
| 009 7-seat AWD | $135,900 |
| 009 6-seat AWD | $139,900 |

Carsales gives an indicative drive-away range of $122,218 to $153,907, which varies by state [3].

The FWD has a single 250 kW motor and more range than the AWD: 604 km WLTP against 582 km. The AWD has 450 kW and reaches 100 km/h in 4.5 s against the FWD's 7.9 s [1][2].

## Luxury car tax

Every 009 grade is above the FY2026-27 luxury car tax threshold for fuel-efficient vehicles, $91,661 from 1 July 2026, so LCT applies to the amount above it [4][5]. See [finance](../../buying/finance.md) for more.

## Commercial use

Cars used for rental, rideshare, taxi, courier, bus or tour operations are excluded from Zeekr's standard warranty. Other commercial vehicles are covered for 5 years or 200,000 km, with the battery covered for 8 years or 150,000 km [6].
