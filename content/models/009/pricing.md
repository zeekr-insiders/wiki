## Who is this for?

-   **Private:** Large families who refuse to buy a Kia Carnival and want S-Class luxury.
-   **Commercial:** Limousine drivers, Hotel shuttles. The 5-year warranty drops to **3 Years / 120,000km** for commercial use.

## Insider Value Check
Compared to a **Lexus LM 350h ($160k - $220k)** or a **Mercedes V-Class**, the 009 is a bargain.

-   **Speed:** The 009 is significantly faster.
-   **Tech:** The rear screen and Yamaha audio blow the legacy brands away.
-   **Range:** 582km is enough for almost any metro limo duty cycle.
