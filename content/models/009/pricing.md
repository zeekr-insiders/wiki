# Zeekr 009 Pricing & Positioning

*Last reviewed: August 2026.*

## Australian Pricing (Pre-ORC)

| Variant | Price |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Zeekr 009 7-Seat FWD** | **$115,900** |
| **Zeekr 009 7-Seat AWD** | **$135,900** |
| **Zeekr 009 6-Seat AWD** | **$139,900** |

The FWD entry variant joined the range for 2026. Its single 250kW motor gives up the AWD's pace (0-100 in 7.9s vs 4.5s), but it has the longest range in the line-up at up to 604km WLTP.

## Who is this for?

- **Private:** Large families who refuse to buy a Kia Carnival and want S-Class luxury.
- **Commercial:** Limousine drivers, hotel shuttles. The 5-year warranty drops to **3 Years / 120,000km** for commercial use.

## Insider Value Check

Compared to a **Lexus LM ($173,814-$236,814 drive-away)**, the 009 makes a strong value case - and the FWD widens the gap further. Against a **Mercedes V-Class** (from about $129k drive-away, diesel), the AWD 009 is similar money but a very different proposition.

- **Speed:** The AWD 009 is significantly faster than anything in the class.
- **Tech:** The rear screen and Yamaha audio are a generation ahead of the legacy brands.
- **Range:** 582km (AWD) or up to 604km (FWD) is enough for almost any metro limo duty cycle.
