# Zeekr 009 - Specifications

**The Executive MPV.**

*Last reviewed: August 2026.*

## Dimensions

- **Length:** 5,209 mm (It's huge. Check your garage depth.)
- **Width:** 2,024 mm (Excl. Mirrors)
- **Height:** 1,812 mm
- **Wheelbase:** 3,205 mm

## Powertrain

### FWD (Single Motor, 7-seat)

- **Power:** 250 kW / 373 Nm
- **0-100 km/h:** 7.9 Seconds
- **Range (WLTP):** Up to 604 km (longest in the range)

### AWD (Dual Motor, 6 or 7-seat)

- **Power:** 450 kW / 693 Nm
- **0-100 km/h:** 4.5 Seconds
- **Battery:** 116 kWh NMC
- **Range (WLTP):** 582 km

Note: the 009 is **not rated for towing** in Australia.

## Seating Configurations

### 7-Seat (2+2+3)

- **Middle Row:** "Sofaro" Captain's Chairs (Heated/Ventilated/Massage). Centre aisle for access to rear.
- **Rear Row:** 3-seat bench (60:40 split).

### 6-Seat (2+2+2) - "The Executive"

- **Middle Row:** Enhanced "Sofaro" Aviation Seats.
    - Wider, plusher.
    - Leg Rests & Footrests.
    - Hidden Tray Tables.
    - No centre aisle (Access to rear via electric slide).

## Key Luxury Features

- **Air Suspension:** Standard (Dual Chamber + CDC).
- **Rear Screen:** 17-inch 3K OLED (Folds down from roof).
- **Audio:** 30-Speaker Yamaha Surround (3000W).
- **Doors:** Power Sliding Side Doors + Power Tailgate.
- **Quietness:** Active Road Noise Cancellation (RNC).

## Charging

The 009 uses a 400V platform, so it can't make full use of 350kW ultra-rapid chargers.

| Method | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **DC Fast Charge (10-80%)** | ~30 Minutes. Zeekr hasn't published an official peak DC rate - reports vary between 150kW and ~205kW |
| **AC Charge** | 11kW / 22kW (variant dependent) |
| **V2L (Vehicle-to-Load)** | 3.3kW output |

## Warranty & Roadside Assist

| Coverage | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Vehicle Warranty** | 5 Years / Unlimited km |
| **Battery Warranty** | 8 Years / 160,000 km (>70% capacity) |
| **Roadside Assistance** | 5 Years complimentary |
