# 2025 Zeekr X Specifications (Australia)

**The Compact Luxury SUV.**

## Dimensions
-   **Length:** 4,432 mm
-   **Width:** 1,836 mm
-   **Height:** 1,566 mm
-   **Wheelbase:** 2,750 mm
-   **Boot Capacity:** 362L (Seats Up) / 1,182L (Seats Down)

## Powertrain

### RWD (Single Motor)
-   **Power:** 200 kW
-   **Torque:** 343 Nm
-   **0-100 km/h:** 5.6 seconds
-   **Range (WLTP):** 440 km
-   **Battery:** 66 kWh NMC

### AWD (Dual Motor)
-   **Power:** 315 kW (Combined)
-   **Torque:** 543 Nm (Combined)
-   **0-100 km/h:** 3.8 seconds
-   **Range (WLTP):** 400 km
-   **Battery:** 66 kWh NMC

## Charging

| Method | RWD | AWD |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **DC Fast Charge (10-80%)** | ~30 Mins (150kW) | ~30 Mins (150kW) |
| **AC Slow Charge** | 7kW / 11kW | **22kW (Standard)** |

!!! tip "The 22kW Advantage"
    The AWD model's 22kW AC charger is a rarity. If you have access to 3-phase power (industrial units, some public posts), you can charge 0-100% in just **3 hours** on AC.

## Key Features

### Standard (RWD)
-   14.6" Sliding Center Screen (Optional in some markets, check local spec)
-   Panormaic Glass Roof
-   Wireless Phone Charger
-   360-Degree Camera

### Convenience Package (Standard on AWD / +$4k on RWD)
*This is the "Must Have" pack.*
-   **Audio:** Yamaha 13-Speaker Surround Sound.
-   **HUD:** 24.3" AR Heads-Up Display.
-   **Seats:** Ventilated Front Seats, Heated Steering Wheel.
-   **Ambient Light:** "Ice Block" Ambient Lighting.
