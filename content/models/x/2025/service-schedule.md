# Zeekr X servicing and warranty

*Last checked: 8 November 2026.*

## Service interval (MY26)

The updated 2026 Zeekr X is serviced every **12 months or 20,000 km**, down from 24 months or 40,000 km [^1][^2]. Capped-price servicing runs for up to 8 years or 160,000 km [^1]:

| Grade | 5 years | 8 years |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| X RWD | $2,662 | $4,766 |
| X AWD | $3,072 | $5,586 |

!!! note "MY25 owners: check your booklet"
    No source we found says whether MY25 cars moved to the shorter interval. Your Service and Warranty Booklet is the authority [^3].

## Warranty

These are Zeekr's standard terms for private-use cars [^3]:

| Cover | Term |
| :--- | :--- |
| Vehicle warranty | 5 years, unlimited km |
| High-voltage battery | 8 years or 160,000 km; repair or replacement if state of health falls below 70% |
| Corrosion (perforation) and paint | 5 years |
| Roadside assistance | 5 years |
| 12-volt battery and key fob battery | 12 months or 20,000 km |
| Accessories fitted after delivery | 24 months or 20,000 km |

If you bought under a pre-order or launch offer, your contract sets your terms; the warranty policy says promotional offers can vary them [^3].

## Keeping your warranty

Zeekr's warranty policy does not say you must service at a Zeekr dealer. What it does say [^3]:

- The car must be serviced and maintained to the Zeekr schedule in the Service and Warranty Booklet, using parts that meet Zeekr's specifications.
- Keep detailed records of each service: date, odometer, work done, parts and fluids, and the job card. You need to show the booklet when you claim.
- Faults caused by servicing that was not done to Zeekr's specifications, or not done by a "duly certified and qualified" workshop, are excluded.
- Warranty claims are made through an authorised Zeekr dealer. Report a fault within 7 calendar days, or as soon as practicable.

The ACCC's position is that a manufacturer's warranty typically lets you choose your own repairer for servicing, as long as the manufacturer's servicing standards are met; some warranties require repairs claimed under warranty to be done in the dealer network [^4]. Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law apply in addition to the warranty, and Zeekr's policy says it does not limit them [^3].

## Roadside assistance

Zeekr Roadside is on **1800 093 357**, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, anywhere in Australia [^5][^6]. Cover includes phone help, a flat 12-volt battery test or jump-start, a flat-tyre repair or tow to a tyre repairer, help with lost or locked-in keys, and a tow to the nearest authorised Zeekr retailer [^6]. If the high-voltage battery is flat, a tow is the likely outcome: ask when you call.
