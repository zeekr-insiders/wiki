## What's Included in a Service?

A typical Zeekr major service includes:

- Multi-point vehicle inspection
- Brake system check (pads, fluid, rotors)
- Suspension and steering inspection
- Tyre condition and pressure check
- Battery health diagnostic
- Thermal management system check
- Cabin filter inspection/replacement
- Software updates if available
- Fluid level checks and top-ups

## Common Maintenance Items

**Cabin Filter:** High-grade filter, replaced during services if needed. ~$60-100.

**Wiper Blades:** Frameless design - quality replacements recommended. ~$40-70 per set.

**Brake Fluid:** Flush typically at 4-year service or as needed.

**Tyres:** The X's compact size and good weight distribution means decent tyre life. Expect 40,000-50,000km from the factory tyres with normal driving. Budget $250-350 per tyre for quality replacements.

**12V Battery:** Small battery for accessories. Lasts 4-6 years typically. ~$150-250 replacement.

## Maintaining Your Warranty

To keep your warranty valid:

- **Service on schedule:** Don't miss your 2-year/40,000km services
- **Use authorised dealers:** Services must be done at Zeekr dealerships
- **Keep records:** Save all service receipts and documentation
- **Report issues promptly:** Don't delay on warranty claims

## Running Costs

The X's smaller size translates to lower running costs:

**Servicing:** $690 every 2 years = $345/year

**Electricity:** ~$15-25/week for typical urban driving (vs $70-90 for equivalent petrol SUV)

**Tyres:** Smaller tyres = lower replacement costs vs larger EVs

**Insurance:** Typically lower premiums than larger/more expensive EVs

!!! tip "Lower Insurance"
    The Zeekr X's smaller size and competitive pricing often translates to lower insurance premiums compared to larger EVs. Shop around and mention it's an EV - some insurers offer EV-specific discounts.

## Service Centre Tips

**What to bring:**

- Driver's licence
- Service booking confirmation
- List of any issues or concerns

**What to expect:**

- Service takes 2-4 hours typically
- Many dealers offer courtesy vehicles (book ahead)
- You'll get a written report of work done

**Pro tip:** Book your service 2-4 weeks in advance. Service centres are getting busier as more Zeekrs hit the road.
