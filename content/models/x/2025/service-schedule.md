# Zeekr X servicing and warranty

*Last checked: 15 November 2026.*

## Service interval

The Zeekr X is serviced every **12 months or 20,000 km**, whichever comes first [^1][^2][^3]. Cars from the September 2024 launch were on 24 months or 40,000 km [^4].

!!! note "Early MY25 owners: check your booklet"
    The 12-month interval is usually described as new for the MY26 car [^1], but an MY25 X reviewed in August 2025 was already on it [^3]. Your Service and Warranty Booklet sets the interval for your car [^5].

## What each service includes

Zeekr Australia doesn't publish the X's service list online. Zeekr's schedules for Malaysia and Singapore, on the same 12-month or 20,000 km interval, follow this pattern [^6][^7]:

| Service | Replaced |
| :--- | :--- |
| Every service | Cabin (A/C) filter, plus a maintenance inspection |
| Every 2 years or 40,000 km | Brake fluid and reduction gear oil (rear on RWD, front and rear on AWD) |
| Every 4 years or 80,000 km | Coolant |

They are a guide to what your dealer will do, not an Australian promise. The Singapore manual halves the brake fluid and cabin filter intervals in harsh conditions such as dust, hilly or humid areas and extreme temperatures [^7].

None of these schedules list tyre rotation, wiper blades, an air-conditioning regas or a key fob battery [^6][^7]. Ask for a tyre rotation when you book if you want one.

## Capped-price servicing

### MY26

Capped-price servicing for the MY26 X runs for up to 8 years or 160,000 km [^1]:

| Grade | 5 years | 8 years |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| X RWD | $2,662 | $4,766 |
| X AWD | $3,072 | $5,586 |

The odd-year services (1st, 3rd, 5th, 7th) are $279 on both grades; the even-year services cost more, up to $1,450 on the AWD [^1]. The first two services together are valued at $829 (RWD) or $1,064 (AWD) [^8][^9], so the second service is roughly $550 (RWD) or $785 (AWD) (calculated from those values). Confirm the price when you book.

### MY25

Capped prices for the MY25 X AWD over the first five years or 100,000 km [^3]:

| Service | 1st | 2nd | 3rd | 4th | 5th |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| X AWD | $237 | $806 | $237 | $1,535 | $237 |

The RWD is $1,090 over three years and $2,532 over five [^3]. The AWD's five-year total is quoted as $2,862, less than the $3,052 the services above add up to [^3], so check the figure for your car with your dealer.

## Free servicing offers

- **April 2025, existing owners.** When Zeekr cut the X's prices, it offered existing owners free servicing: three years on the AWD and five years on the RWD [^10][^11]. Confirm what applies to your car with your dealer.
- **July 2026, new MY26 cars.** Orders from 1 to 31 July 2026 got the first two scheduled services free, worth $829 (RWD) or $1,064 (AWD). Private retail buyers only; fleet, government, rideshare, ABN and rental buyers were excluded. Servicing had to follow the Service and Warranty Booklet at an authorised dealer, and anything extra was charged [^8][^9].

As at 15 November 2026, Zeekr's offers page lists no current offer for the X [^12].

## Warranty

These are Zeekr's standard terms for private-use cars [^5]:

| Cover | Term |
| :--- | :--- |
| Vehicle warranty | 5 years, unlimited km |
| High-voltage battery | 8 years or 160,000 km; repair or replacement if state of health falls below 70% |
| Corrosion (perforation) and paint | 5 years |
| Roadside assistance | 5 years |
| 12-volt battery and key fob battery | 12 months or 20,000 km |
| Accessories fitted after delivery | 24 months or 20,000 km |

If you bought under a pre-order or launch offer, your contract sets your terms; the warranty policy says promotional offers can vary them [^5].

## Keeping your warranty

Zeekr's warranty policy does not say you must service at a Zeekr dealer. What it does say [^5]:

- The car must be serviced and maintained to the Zeekr schedule in the Service and Warranty Booklet, using parts that meet Zeekr's specifications.
- Keep detailed records of each service: date, odometer, work done, parts and fluids, and the job card. You need to show the booklet when you claim.
- Faults caused by servicing that was not done to Zeekr's specifications, or not done by a "duly certified and qualified" workshop, are excluded.
- Warranty claims are made through an authorised Zeekr dealer. Report a fault within 7 calendar days, or as soon as practicable.

The ACCC's position is that a manufacturer's warranty typically lets you choose your own repairer for servicing, as long as the manufacturer's servicing standards are met; some warranties require repairs claimed under warranty to be done in the dealer network [^13]. Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law apply in addition to the warranty, and Zeekr's policy says it does not limit them [^5].

## Roadside assistance

Zeekr Roadside is on **1800 093 357**, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, anywhere in Australia [^14][^15]. Cover includes phone help, a flat 12-volt battery test or jump-start, a flat-tyre repair or tow to a tyre repairer, help with lost or locked-in keys, and a tow to the nearest authorised Zeekr retailer [^15]. If the high-voltage battery is flat, a tow is the likely outcome: ask when you call.
