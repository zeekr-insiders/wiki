# Recommended Accessories

Accessories recommended by {{ extra.club.full_name }} members based on real ownership experience. These are practical items that owners have found genuinely useful.

**Important:** These are owner recommendations, not {{ extra.club.abbreviation }} endorsements. Your needs and preferences may differ. Always verify compatibility with your specific Zeekr model and year.

## Interior Accessories

### Floor Protection

**All-Weather Floor Mats**

- **Why:** Protect factory carpet from dirt, mud, and spills
- **What to look for:** Custom-fit for your model, raised edges, easy to clean
- **Brands mentioned:** WeatherTech, Husky Liners, TuxMat, 3D MAXpider
- **Cost:** $200-$400 for full set (front and rear)
- **Note:** Ensure driver's mat has proper retention clips to prevent interference with pedals

**Boot Liner / Cargo Mat**

- **Why:** Keeps boot pristine, especially useful for groceries, sports equipment, pets
- **What to look for:** Full coverage, waterproof, raised edges
- **Options:** Fitted rubber mat or heavy-duty cargo tray
- **Cost:** $100-$250
- **Tip:** Some owners add a second layer of removable mat for extra protection

### Organisation

**Centre Console Organiser**

- **Why:** Prevents items from sliding around, maximises usable space
- **What to look for:** Custom-fit inserts with compartments for phone, cards, coins
- **Cost:** $30-$80
- **Note:** Measure your centre console - generic organisers may not fit properly

**Boot Organisers**

- **Why:** Keeps cargo from shifting, especially useful for charging cables
- **Options:** Collapsible storage boxes, cargo nets, dividers
- **Cost:** $30-$100
- **Tip:** Dedicated spot for your portable charging cable keeps it clean and accessible

### Comfort

**Sunshades**

- **Why:** Essential for Australian climate - keeps interior cooler when parked
- **Options:**
  - Windscreen sunshade (folding or roller style): $20-$60
  - Side window shades (magnetic or static cling): $30-$80 per set
  - Custom-fit full-car shade (interior or exterior): $200-$500
- **Tip:** Windscreen shade is the minimum, side shades for rear passengers highly recommended

**Seat Covers (Selective Use)**

- **Why:** Protect leather/fabric from wear, spills, pet damage
- **Consideration:** Zeekr seats are high quality - only cover if you have specific needs (pets, kids, work use)
- **Cost:** $100-$500 depending on coverage and quality
- **Note:** Ensure covers don't interfere with side airbags

## Exterior Accessories

### Protection

**Door Edge Guards**

- **Why:** Prevent chips and dings when opening doors in tight spaces
- **Options:** Clear film, colour-matched, or rubber bumpers
- **Cost:** $20-$80
- **Tip:** Often included in PPF packages, check before purchasing separately

**Mud Flaps / Splash Guards**

- **Why:** Reduce paint damage from stones and debris kicked up by tyres
- **Consideration:** More important if you drive on gravel or rural roads frequently
- **Cost:** $80-$200 installed
- **Note:** Some owners feel they detract from the clean lines; personal preference

**Door Handle Protection Film**

- **Why:** Prevents scratches around handles from fingernails and rings
- **Options:** Clear PPF or carbon fibre effect
- **Cost:** $30-$80 (often DIY installation)
- **Tip:** Apply carefully to avoid bubbles - or have it done during PPF installation

### Styling (Taste Dependent)

**Wheel Accessories**

- **Centre cap covers** - Zeekr logo or custom ($30-$100)
- **Wheel spacers** - Wider stance (check warranty implications)
- **Aftermarket wheels** - Personalisation ($$$ and declare to insurance)

**Note:** Be cautious with modifications that affect handling or safety. Always check warranty implications.

## Charging Accessories

### Home Charging

**Wall-Mounted Cable Holder**

- **Why:** Keeps charging cable organised and off the ground
- **Options:** Simple hooks ($20-$40) or purpose-built cable management systems ($80-$150)
- **Tip:** Install near your home charger for convenience

**Cable Bag/Organiser**

- **Why:** Keeps portable charging cable clean and tangle-free in the boot
- **Options:** Soft storage bags or hard cases
- **Cost:** $30-$80
- **Tip:** Look for water-resistant material to protect connector

**Charging Extension Lead (10A Portable Charging)**

- **Why:** Provides flexibility when using 10A portable charger
- **Specifications:** 10A-rated heavy-duty extension, outdoor-rated if needed
- **Cost:** $30-$80 for quality cable
- **Warning:** Must be 10A rated minimum, ideally 15A. Never daisy-chain extensions.

### Public Charging

**Charging Network Memberships**

- **Chargefox** - Largest network in Australia
- **Evie Networks** - Growing ultra-fast network
- **NRMA (NSW/ACT)** - Regional coverage
- **Check app for current pricing** - Some offer subscription plans with lower per-kWh rates

**Charging Adapters**

- **Why:** Compatibility with various charger types
- **Common:** Type 2 to Type 1 adapter (rare need in Australia)
- **Cost:** $100-$300
- **Note:** Most Zeekr models use Type 2, which is the Australian standard. Adapters rarely needed.

**Screen Cleaning Wipes**

- **Why:** Touchscreen gets fingerprints, especially after plugging in chargers
- **What to use:** Microfibre cloths and screen-safe cleaning solution
- **Cost:** $10-$30
- **Tip:** Keep a microfibre cloth in the car

## Safety & Security

### Security

**Dashcam**

- **Why:** Evidence in case of accidents, insurance claims, vandalism
- **Features to consider:**
  - Front and rear cameras
  - Parking mode (records when parked)
  - Night vision
  - GPS logging
  - Impact detection (G-sensor)
- **Brands mentioned:** BlackVue, Viofo, Thinkware, Nextbase
- **Cost:** $200-$600+ depending on features
- **Installation:** DIY or professional hardwiring ($100-$200) for parking mode
- **Note:** Some Zeekr models may have built-in dashcam functionality - check first

**GPS Tracker (Optional)**

- **Why:** Theft recovery, fleet management
- **Consideration:** Most Zeekr vehicles have built-in tracking via the Zeekr app
- **Aftermarket:** Consider if you want independent tracking or advanced features
- **Cost:** $100-$300 plus subscription fees

### Emergency Kit

**Portable Tyre Inflator**

- **Why:** EVs often don't carry spare tyres, just repair kits
- **Features:** 12V plug-in, compact, digital pressure gauge
- **Cost:** $50-$150
- **Tip:** Check your Zeekr's included emergency kit before purchasing

**Emergency Roadside Kit**

- **Contents:** Jumper cables (for 12V battery), torch, first aid kit, reflective triangle, high-vis vest
- **Cost:** $50-$150 for comprehensive kit
- **Note:** EVs can still have 12V battery issues even if the main battery is fine

## Cleaning & Maintenance

### Washing

**Microfibre Wash Kit**

- **What:** Wash mitts, drying towels, detail sprays
- **Why:** Prevent scratches during washing, especially important with PPF or ceramic coating
- **Cost:** $50-$150 for quality kit
- **Brands:** Bowden's Own, Meguiar's, Chemical Guys, Gtechniq

**pH-Neutral Car Wash**

- **Why:** Essential if you have ceramic coating - preserves the coating
- **Cost:** $20-$40 per bottle (lasts many washes)
- **Tip:** Avoid dish soap or harsh detergents

**Wheel Cleaner**

- **Why:** Brake dust and road grime on wheels needs specific cleaner
- **What to look for:** pH-neutral, safe for all wheel finishes
- **Cost:** $15-$30
- **Tip:** Some ceramic coatings can be applied to wheels for easier cleaning

### Interior Care

**Interior Detailer Spray**

- **Why:** Quick clean for dashboard, screens, and surfaces
- **Cost:** $15-$30
- **Tip:** Use separate cloth for screens vs other surfaces to avoid streaking

**Leather Conditioner (If Applicable)**

- **Why:** Keeps leather seats supple and prevents cracking
- **Frequency:** Every 3-6 months depending on climate
- **Cost:** $20-$50
- **Note:** Check if your seats are leather or synthetic - treatment differs

## Tech Accessories

### Phone & Connectivity

**Phone Mount (If Needed)**

- **Why:** Some prefer dedicated mount over built-in wireless charging pad
- **Types:** Vent mount, dashboard mount, MagSafe compatible
- **Cost:** $20-$80
- **Note:** Check if your Zeekr model has wireless charging pad that holds phone securely

**USB Charging Adapters**

- **Why:** Additional charging ports for passengers
- **What:** USB-C or USB-A multi-port adapter for 12V socket
- **Cost:** $15-$40
- **Tip:** Look for fast-charging compatible adapters

### Convenience

**Portable Jump Starter (For 12V Battery)**

- **Why:** 12V battery failure can prevent access to vehicle, even with full main battery
- **Features:** Compact, USB charging, built-in torch
- **Cost:** $80-$200
- **Note:** Store in house or garage, not in car (extreme temperatures affect battery packs)

## What to Avoid

Based on community experience, accessories that are often not worth it:

- **Cheap PPF or tint** - Poor quality fails quickly and damages paint on removal
- **"Miracle" coatings** - If it sounds too good to be true, it probably is
- **Universal-fit mats** - Poor fit allows dirt to reach carpet
- **Decorative chrome trim** - Personal taste, but many find it dates the look
- **Excessive badging or decals** - Less is more for resale value
- **Performance chips/tuners** - No proven benefit for EVs, may void warranty

## Before You Buy

**Check compatibility:**

- Verify fit for your specific Zeekr model and year
- Read reviews from other owners if possible
- Check return policy if purchasing online

**Consider necessity:**

- Will this genuinely improve your ownership experience?
- Is there a simpler/cheaper alternative?
- Can you wait and see if you actually need it?

**Insurance and warranty:**

- Declare significant modifications to your insurer (see [Insurance Guide](../insurance.md))
- Check if modification affects manufacturer warranty
- Keep receipts for insurance purposes
