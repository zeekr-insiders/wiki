# Recommended accessories

Practical accessories that {{ extra.club.full_name }} members have found useful. These are owner suggestions, not {{ extra.club.abbreviation }} endorsements. Always check fitment for your model and model year before you buy: the MY27 7X has a revised centre console, so console organisers and some mats made for the MY25/MY26 car may not fit [^2].

## Interior

### Floor and boot protection

- **All-weather floor mats:** look for a fit made for your model, raised edges and retention clips on the driver's mat so it can't slide under the pedals.
- **Boot liner or cargo tray:** full coverage with raised edges keeps groceries, sports gear and pets off the carpet.

### Organisation

- **Centre console organiser:** measure the console first. Generic inserts often don't fit, and the MY27 7X console layout differs from the MY25/MY26 car [^2].
- **Boot organisers:** collapsible boxes, cargo nets or dividers stop cargo shifting and give your portable charging cable a clean, dry home.

### Comfort

- **Sunshades:** a windscreen shade keeps the cabin cooler when parked. Side shades help rear passengers.
- **Seat covers:** only worth it for pets, kids or work use. Make sure covers don't block side airbags.

## Exterior

- **Door edge guards and handle film:** stop chips and scratches. These are often included in a PPF package, so check before buying separately. See [Protection](protection.md).
- **Mud flaps:** reduce stone damage to lower paintwork, mainly useful if you drive on gravel often.
- **Wheel spacers and aftermarket wheels:** these change handling and may affect warranty and insurance. Declare them to your insurer and read the warranty exclusions first [^3].

## Charging

### Portable charger safety

!!! danger "No extension leads or adapters"
    Zeekr's 7X owner manual says not to use any adapters, extension cables or multiple ports of any kind when charging [^1]. Plug a portable charger straight into a suitable wall socket. If the nearest socket is too far away or isn't suitable, have a licensed electrician install a dedicated outlet or a wallbox.

!!! note "Not yet confirmed for the Australian manual"
    The manual we read is the Netherlands edition of the 7X manual, on an unofficial mirror [^1]. Check the booklet that came with your car, and follow your portable charger's own manual too.

The same manual also says not to use a damaged cable or socket, not to coil or bundle the cable while charging, and to keep the cable out of direct sunlight, because overheating protection can slow or stop charging [^1].

### Storage

- **Wall-mounted cable holder:** keeps the home charging cable off the ground.
- **Cable bag:** keeps the portable cable clean in the boot. Water-resistant material protects the connector.

## Safety and security

### Dashcam

The MY27 7X has a built-in digital video recorder (DVR) as standard on every grade, which uses the car's own cameras and monitors the car while parked [^2]. No source we found says the MY25/MY26 car has one or will get one by software update.

If you fit an aftermarket dashcam to an MY25/MY26 car, use a plug-in power source or have it installed professionally. Zeekr's warranty policy excludes faults caused by unapproved electrical modifications [^3], so don't tap into fuses or wiring yourself.

### Emergency kit

- **Tyre inflator:** check which tyre repair kit your car came with first.
- **Basic roadside kit:** torch, first aid kit, reflective triangle and hi-vis vest.
- **12-volt jump starter:** a flat 12-volt battery can stop the car even when the main battery is full. Zeekr Roadside covers 12-volt battery tests and jump-starts [^4], so this is a backup, not a necessity.

## Cleaning

- **Microfibre wash kit:** wash mitts and drying towels reduce swirl marks, which matters more if you have PPF or ceramic coating.
- **pH-neutral car wash:** recommended for coated paint. Avoid dish soap.
- **Interior care:** use a separate cloth for screens. Check whether your seats are leather or synthetic before using conditioner.

## What to avoid

- Cheap PPF or tint, which can fail early and damage paint on removal
- Universal-fit mats that leave gaps
- Performance chips or "tuners", which have no proven benefit and may affect warranty [^3]

## Before you buy

- Check fitment for your model and model year
- Check the return policy if buying online
- Declare significant modifications to your insurer (see the [Insurance guide](../insurance.md))
- Keep receipts
