# I've just got my car

*Last checked: 8 November 2026.*

The things worth sorting out in your first few weeks with a Zeekr. Some details below are checked against a 7X manual; X and 009 owners should check their own manual where it says so.

## Save these numbers

- **Zeekr Roadside, 24/7:** 1800 093 357 [1]. Have your VIN, your location (nearest cross street), what's wrong and a contact number ready.
- **Zeekr customer support:** the same number, 9 am to 6 pm AEST, Monday to Friday; or customersupportau@zeekrlife.com [1].
- **Your dealer's service desk:** find it on the [dealers page](../buying/dealers.md).

Roadside is Zeekr's own service, not a motoring club membership, and runs for 5 years from first registration [2][3]. What it covers is on the [National page](../resources/national/index.md).

## Know which car you have

7X owners: the updated MY27 delivers from December 2026, and some advice differs between it and the launch car. [Which 7X do I have?](../models/7x/which-7x.md) shows how to tell.

## Keep your paperwork

- Keep your Service and Warranty Booklet with the car. Zeekr's warranty requires servicing to its schedule with records of each service, and you need the booklet to make a claim [2].
- If something goes wrong, report it to an authorised Zeekr dealer within 7 calendar days, or as soon as practicable [2].
- Bought under a launch or pre-order offer (such as extra warranty or a free wallbox)? Keep the offer terms with your contract: they set what you are owed [2].

## Check your service interval

The 7X interval is disputed in published reports (12 months/20,000 km or 24 months/40,000 km), so look it up in your booklet now rather than when the first service is due. The X (MY26) is 12 months/20,000 km. See the service pages for the [7X](../models/7x/2025/service-schedule.md), [X](../models/x/2025/service-schedule.md) and [009](../models/009/service-schedule.md).

## Charging at home

- Plug the portable charger straight into a suitable wall socket. The 7X manual says not to use any adapters, extension cables or multiple ports [4]. If the socket is too far away or not up to it, get a licensed electrician to install a dedicated outlet or a wallbox.
- MY27 launch-offer wallboxes are supply only; installation is at your cost [5].
- More in [home charging](../guides/charging/home-setup.md).

## Settings worth knowing (7X)

These are from a 7X manual (Netherlands edition, unofficial mirror), so the Australian car may differ [6]:

- **Driver drowsiness and distraction alerts** are on by default. Each can be turned off under Settings > Safety & Service > Safety, or from the swipe-down menu.
- **Over-speed alert** warns at about 1 km/h over the limit and is back on at the start of every journey. Change it under Settings > Zeekr AD > Driving assist > Over speed alert; the audible alert can be turned off from the swipe-down menu.
- **Frozen screens:** hold the left steering wheel thumbstick and the right scroll wheel together for ten seconds to restart both displays.

More on these in [7X known issues](../models/7x/knowledge-base/7x-issues.md).
