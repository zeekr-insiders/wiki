# First 30 Days of Ownership

Congratulations on your new Zeekr! The first month is an exciting time to learn your vehicle and get everything set up properly. This guide covers the essential steps to take after delivery.

## Day 1: Delivery Day

### At the Dealership

- Complete a thorough pre-delivery inspection with the dealer
- Document any cosmetic issues immediately with photos
- Ensure all accessories ordered are present and fitted
- Verify the vehicle identification number (VIN) matches your paperwork
- Test all features: windows, climate control, infotainment, cameras
- Collect your keys, charging cable, and any included accessories
- Confirm your next service date and what's covered under warranty

### Before You Drive Away

- Adjust seat, mirrors, and steering wheel position
- Pair your phone via Bluetooth
- Set up a temporary home address in the navigation
- Understand basic controls: drive modes, regenerative braking, parking
- Know how to open the charge port

## Week 1: Essential Setup

### Download and Configure Apps

**Zeekr App** (Essential)

- Download from App Store or Google Play
- Create account and register your vehicle
- Enable notifications for charging status and vehicle alerts
- Explore remote features: climate pre-conditioning, lock/unlock, charging control
- Set up your home location for automatic charging schedules

**Additional Apps to Consider**

- **PlugShare** - Find public charging stations
- **Chargefox** - Major Australian charging network
- **NRMA** - Roadside assistance (if applicable)
- **Your energy provider's app** - Monitor home charging costs

### Configure Vehicle Settings

Take time to explore and customise:

- **Driver Profile** - Save your seat, mirror, and steering positions
- **Climate Preferences** - Set preferred cabin temperature
- **Regenerative Braking** - Start with medium, adjust to preference
- **Charging Limits** - Set to 80% for daily use (if not already default)
- **Display Settings** - Brightness, theme, units (metric)
- **Driver Assistance** - Configure lane keeping, adaptive cruise, parking aids
- **Ambient Lighting** - Personalise interior lighting colours and brightness

### Home Charging Setup

- Install a dedicated EV charger if not already done (see [Charging Guide](../guides/charging/home-setup.md))
- Set charging schedule to take advantage of off-peak electricity rates
- Configure charging limit to 80% for daily use, 100% only for long trips
- Test the included portable charger with a standard 10A outlet (slow, but works)

## Week 2: Learn Your Car

### Driving Modes

Experiment with different drive modes to understand their behaviour:

- **Eco** - Maximum efficiency, gentler acceleration
- **Comfort** - Balanced performance for daily driving
- **Sport** - Full performance, firmer suspension (if equipped)

### Regenerative Braking

- Start with medium regenerative braking
- Practice one-pedal driving in safe conditions
- Adjust to your preference (low, medium, high)
- Learn how it changes in different drive modes

### Range Management

- Monitor your real-world efficiency (kWh/100km)
- Understand how climate control affects range
- Plan charging stops for longer trips using the navigation system
- Don't stress about range anxiety - it decreases with experience

### Advanced Features

Take time to learn and practice:

- **Adaptive Cruise Control** - Highway driving assistance
- **Lane Keeping Assist** - How sensitive is it? Can you adjust it?
- **360° Camera** - Essential for tight parking
- **Parking Assist** - Automatic parking (if equipped)
- **Voice Commands** - Hands-free control of common functions

## Week 3-4: Optimisation

### Review Your Charging Habits

- Are you charging at the most cost-effective times?
- Is your home charger sufficient, or do you need an upgrade?
- Have you located convenient public chargers for your regular routes?

### Insurance Finalisation

- Ensure your insurance company has all correct details
- Confirm agreed value is documented in writing
- Check coverage includes EV-specific items (battery, charging cable)

### Service Planning

## Common Questions

### Breaking In Your EV

Unlike petrol cars, EVs don't require a traditional break-in period. However, some owners prefer to:

- Drive gently for the first 1,000km
- Avoid extreme performance use until tyres are properly scrubbed in
- Allow battery pack to complete several charge cycles
- Let brake pads bed in gradually

**Note:** Modern EVs are tested extensively at the factory. Normal driving from day one is perfectly acceptable.

### Should I Charge to 100%?

- **Daily use:** Charge to 80% to maximise battery longevity
- **Long trips:** Charge to 100% when you need the range
- **Infrequent driving:** If car sits for days, 50-80% is ideal storage range

### How Often Should I Charge?

- Charge when convenient, typically at home overnight
- No need to let battery run low before charging
- EVs prefer regular top-ups rather than deep discharge cycles
- Plan your charging around your electricity rates

### What About Software Updates?

- Zeekr releases over-the-air (OTA) updates periodically
- Install updates when prompted (usually takes 15-30 minutes)
- Car must be parked and may require restart after installation
- Updates can add features, improve performance, or fix bugs
