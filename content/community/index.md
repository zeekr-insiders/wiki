# Community

Run by owners, for owners. Independent of Zeekr Australia, and happy to work with them where it helps owners.

Zeekr Insiders is a national community of Zeekr owners, with state leads organising things locally. A volunteer leadership team meets every fortnight to plan drives, events and the club's websites.

## What we do

- **Drives and events.** Coffee meets, family days, pet outings, drives, touring and camping trips run across the country [^1]. See [Drives and events](events.md).
- **This wiki.** Reference material on buying and owning a Zeekr in Australia, with sources on every page.
- **The forum.** For questions and conversations. See [Forum](forum.md).

## Join in

- Find a drive or event on the [Drives page](https://zeekrinsiders.com.au/drives).
- Read the [club guides](https://zeekrinsiders.com.au/garage/guides) before your first drive, including the [code of conduct](https://zeekrinsiders.com.au/garage/guides#code-of-conduct) [^2].
- Want to run something? You don't need to be on the leadership team [^1]: talk to your state lead or post on the forum.
