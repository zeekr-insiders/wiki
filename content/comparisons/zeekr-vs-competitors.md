## The Big One: Zeekr 7X vs Tesla Model Y

This is the comparison most people care about. Tesla dominated the Australian EV market for years (42% market share in 2023), but that's dropped to 30% in 2024 as Chinese brands offer better value. Here's how they stack up.

### <img src="../../assets/icons/icon-price.svg" class="zi-icon" alt=""> Price Comparison

| Model | Drive-Away Price (VIC) | Battery | Range (WLTP) |
|-------|----------------------|---------|--------------|
| **Zeekr 7X Long Range RWD** | ~$68,800 | 100kWh NMC | 615km |
| **Tesla Model Y RWD** | ~$75,000+ | ~60kWh LFP | 533km |
| **Zeekr 7X RWD** | ~$62,500 | 75kWh LFP | 480km |
| **Tesla Model Y Long Range AWD** | ~$90,000+ | ~75kWh NMC | 551km |

**Winner: Zeekr 7X** - You're saving $6,000+ and getting more range. That's not small change.

### <img src="../../assets/icons/icon-charging-speed.svg" class="zi-icon" alt=""> Charging Speed (The Game Changer)

This is where things get interesting.

| Feature | Zeekr 7X | Tesla Model Y |
|---------|----------|---------------|
| **Architecture** | **800V** (Like Porsche Taycan) | 400V (Standard) |
| **Peak DC Speed** | **450kW** (LFP) / 420kW (NMC) | ~170kW |
| **10-80% Time** | **~10.5 min** (ideal conditions) | ~27 minutes |
| **Real-World Highway Stop** | 15-20 minutes | 30-40 minutes |

**Winner: Zeekr 7X** - This isn't even close. The 800V architecture means you actually get coffee-break charging speeds on road trips. Tesla's 400V system is fine for city driving, but the Zeekr absolutely dominates on long trips.

!!! tip "Why 800V Matters"
    Most EVs (including Tesla) use 400V architecture. Zeekr's 800V system can handle twice the voltage, which means faster charging without overheating. Think of it like filling a bucket with a fire hose instead of a garden hose.

### <img src="../../assets/icons/icon-power.svg" class="zi-icon" alt=""> Features & Tech

| Feature | Zeekr 7X Long Range | Tesla Model Y |
|---------|---------------------|---------------|
| **Screen** | 16" 3.5K Centre + Driver Display + AR-HUD | 15.4" Centre Only |
| **Sound System** | 21-Speaker Zeekr Sound Pro | 13-Speaker Premium |
| **Seats** | Massage + Ventilation Standard | Heated Front Only |
| **Sunroof** | Electric Sunshade Included | Fixed Glass (No Shade) |
| **V2L** | Yes (3.5kW) | No |
| **Digital Key** | Physical Key + App | App Only (Known Issues) |

**Winner: Zeekr 7X** - The Long Range trim includes features Tesla charges extra for (or doesn't offer at all). The Tesla's minimalist approach works for some, but most families prefer having a proper driver display and physical controls.

### <img src="../../assets/icons/icon-bootspace.svg" class="zi-icon" alt=""> Practicality

|---------|----------|---------------|

**Winner: Split** - Tesla has more cargo space. Zeekr has better rear seat comfort and higher towing capacity. Pick your priority.

### <img src="../../assets/icons/icon-range.svg" class="zi-icon" alt=""> Real-World Ownership

| Factor | Zeekr 7X | Tesla Model Y |
|--------|----------|---------------|
| **Warranty** | 5yr Unlimited km | 4yr 80,000km |
| **Service Network** | Growing (20+ locations) | Established (30+ locations) |
| **Software Updates** | OTA (Some bugs reported) | OTA (Mature system) |
| **Resale Data** | Unknown (New to market) | Strong track record |
| **Digital Key** | Physical backup included | App only (issues reported) |
| **Build Quality** | Premium feel, tight panel gaps | Mixed reports, quality varies |

**Winner: Tesla (for now)** - Tesla's service network is bigger and their software is more mature. But Zeekr's physical key backup is a huge plus if you've ever been locked out by a Tesla app glitch.

!!! warning "App Reliability Reality Check"
    The Zeekr app has had connectivity issues for some owners. If you rely heavily on remote climate control and digital key, this is frustrating. Tesla's app is more reliable, but when it fails, you're properly stuck.

| **Sound System** | 21-Speaker Zeekr | 12-Speaker Dynaudio |

## Quick Decision Matrix

### Choose Zeekr 7X If...

- You want the fastest charging available (800V)
- You do regular road trips over 300km
- You value features like AR-HUD, massage seats, premium sound
- You want better value than Tesla
- You don't mind a newer brand

### Choose Tesla Model Y If...

- Resale value is critical (more data available)
- You want the most mature software
- You already own a Tesla and like the ecosystem
- The Supercharger network matters to you (shrinking advantage)
- Brand recognition is important

### Choose BYD Sealion 7 If...

- You want the best value per dollar
- Charging speed doesn't matter much
- You mainly do city driving
- You're budget-conscious but want quality

### Choose Zeekr X If...

- You want a compact SUV with hot hatch performance
- The 3.8s 0-100 (AWD) excites you
- You have 3-phase power at home/work (22kW AC charging)
- You prioritise city manoeuvrability

### Choose Zeekr 009 If...

- You need seating for 6-7 in luxury
- You're chauffeur-driven or run an executive service
- You want a statement vehicle
- You value silence and comfort above everything

## The Honest Summary

**Where Zeekr Wins:**

- 800V charging (huge advantage)
- Value for features included
- Premium interior materials
- Range (7X Long Range)
- Warranty (5yr unlimited vs Tesla's 4yr/80k)

**Where Zeekr Loses:**

- Service network (growing but smaller)
- Software maturity (Tesla is better)
- Resale data (unknown, too new)
- App reliability (connectivity issues)
- Brand recognition (matters to some)

**The Bottom Line:**

If you value practical advantages (faster charging, more range, better features) over brand name, Zeekr is the smart choice. If you want the safest resale bet and most mature software, Tesla still has that edge - but it's shrinking fast.

The best advice? Test drive both. The 800V charging difference is real, but only matters if you road trip regularly.
