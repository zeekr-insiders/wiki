## Quick Decision Guide

### Best Value Overall

1. **BYD Atto 3 Extended** (~$44-49k) - Most EV for the money
2. **Zeekr X RWD** ($48,900 drive-away) - Premium compact at near-budget price
3. **BYD Sealion 7 Premium** (~$60-65k) - Best value mid-size

### Best Range

1. **Zeekr 7X Long Range RWD** (615km)
2. **Polestar 3** (610km) - at nearly twice the price
3. **Zeekr 009 FWD** (604km)

### Fastest Charging (Road Trip Champion)

1. **Zeekr 7X** (800V) - 10-80% in 13 min (RWD) / 16 min (LR, AWD)
2. **Polestar 3** (800V, 350kW) - ~22 min
3. **Tesla Model Y LR** (250kW) - ~27 min

### Best Performance (0-100 km/h)

1. **Zeekr X AWD** (3.7s) and **Zeekr 7X AWD** (3.8s)
2. **Zeekr 009 AWD** (4.5s) - remarkable for a near-3-tonne MPV
3. **BYD Sealion 7 Performance** (4.5s)

### Best Features per Dollar

1. **Zeekr 7X Long Range** - AR-HUD, 21 speakers, massage seats at ~$68.8k drive-away
2. **Zeekr X AWD** - 22kW AC, Yamaha sound, 3.7s at $57,900 drive-away
3. **BYD Sealion 7 Premium** - Dynaudio, good features at ~$60-65k

### Best Service Network

1. **Tesla** - mature, national network
2. **BYD** - large and growing dealer network
3. **Zeekr** - ~17 locations and expanding

### Best Warranty

1. **BYD** (6yr / 150k) - Best coverage
2. **Zeekr** (5yr / Unlimited km) - No distance limit
3. **Polestar** (5yr / Unlimited km) - Now matches Zeekr on term

## Use Case Recommendations

### "I need a city car that's fun to drive"

**Top Pick:** Zeekr X AWD ($57,900 drive-away)

- 3.7s 0-100
- 22kW AC charging (rare)
- Compact size, easy parking

**Budget Alternative:** BYD Atto 3 Extended (~$44-49k)

### "I want the best family road-trip EV"

**Top Pick:** Zeekr 7X Long Range RWD (~$68.8k)

- 615km range (longest under $100k)
- 800V ultra-rapid charging (10-80% in ~16 min)
- 21-speaker sound system for happy kids

**Budget Alternative:** BYD Sealion 7 Premium (~$60-65k)

### "I need performance AND practicality"

**Top Pick:** Zeekr 7X AWD Performance (~$78.5k)

- 3.8s 0-100
- 800V charging (road trip ready)
- 2,000kg towing, air suspension

**Premium Alternative:** Tesla Model Y Long Range (~$90k)

### "I want the ultimate luxury shuttle"

**Top Pick:** Zeekr 009 ($115,900-$139,900 + ORC)

- Aviation-style executive seats (6-seat AWD)
- 30 Yamaha speakers
- FWD variant brings the entry price down $20k

**Alternative:** Mercedes V-Class (from ~$129k, diesel)

### "I just want the cheapest EV that's actually good"

**Top Pick:** BYD Atto 3 Extended (~$44-49k)

- Proven reliability
- Good range (420km)
- 6yr/150k warranty

**Step-Up Pick:** Zeekr X RWD ($48,900 drive-away) - More premium for a few grand extra
