## Quick Decision Guide

### Best Value Overall

1. **BYD Atto 3** (~$52k) - Most EV for the money
2. **Zeekr X RWD** (~$54k) - Premium compact at near-budget price
3. **BYD Sealion 7** (~$65k) - Best value mid-size

### Best Range

1. **Zeekr 7X Long Range RWD** (615km) - Range king
2. **Polestar 3** (610km) - Premium alternative
3. **Zeekr 009** (582km) - Luxury MPV range

### Fastest Charging (Road Trip Champion)

1. **Zeekr 7X** (All variants - 800V) - 10-80% in ~10.5 min
2. **BYD Sealion 7** (230kW) - ~30 min
3. **Tesla Model Y LR** (250kW) - ~27 min

### Best Performance (0-100 km/h)

1. **Zeekr X AWD** & **Zeekr 7X AWD** (3.8s) - Supercar pace
2. **Zeekr 009** (4.5s) - Fastest MPV ever
3. **BYD Sealion 7 Excellence** (4.5s) - Budget rocket

### Best Features per Dollar

1. **Zeekr 7X Long Range** - AR-HUD, 21 speakers, massage seats at $68.8k
2. **Zeekr X AWD** - 22kW AC, Yamaha sound, 3.8s at $67.5k
3. **BYD Sealion 7 Premium** - Dynaudio, good features at $65k

### Best Service Network

1. **Tesla** - 30+ locations, mature network

### Best Warranty

1. **BYD** (6yr / 150k) - Best coverage

## Use Case Recommendations

### "I need a city car that's fun to drive"

**Top Pick:** Zeekr X AWD (~$67.5k)

- 3.8s 0-100 (supercar pace)
- 22kW AC charging (rare!)
- Compact size, easy parking

**Budget Alternative:** BYD Atto 3 Extended (~$52k)

### "I want the best family road-trip EV"

**Top Pick:** Zeekr 7X Long Range RWD (~$68.8k)

- 615km range (longest)
- 800V ultra-rapid charging (10-80% in ~10.5 min)
- 21-speaker sound system for happy kids

**Budget Alternative:** BYD Sealion 7 Premium (~$65k)

### "I need performance AND practicality"

**Top Pick:** Zeekr 7X AWD Performance (~$78.5k)

- 3.8s 0-100 (faster than most sports cars)
- 800V charging (road trip ready)
- 2,000kg towing, air suspension

**Premium Alternative:** Tesla Model Y Long Range (~$90k)

### "I want the ultimate luxury shuttle"

**Top Pick:** Zeekr 009 6-Seat (~$145k)

- Aviation-style executive seats
- 30 Yamaha speakers
- 4.5s 0-100 (fastest MPV)

**Budget Alternative:** Mercedes V-Class (~$125k diesel)

### "I just want the cheapest EV that's actually good"

**Top Pick:** BYD Atto 3 Extended (~$52k)

- Proven reliability
- Good range (420km)
- 6yr/150k warranty

**Step-Up Pick:** Zeekr X RWD (~$54k) - More premium for $2k extra
