# Join Zeekr Insiders

**We are the community for Zeekr owners who want more than the brochure.**

This isn't just a social club. It's a collective resource to make ownership better, cheaper, and smarter.

## Why Join?

### 1. Group Buys (The "Zeekr Tax" Avoidance)
Accessories for new car brands are often overpriced or hard to find. We organise bulk orders directly from manufacturers for:
-   **3D Floor Mats:** Essential for Australian winters/beaches.
-   **Screen Protectors:** For that massive 16-inch fingerprint magnet.
-   **V2L Adapters:** If you lost yours or need a spare.
-   **Mud Flaps:** Protect your paint on country roads.

### 2. Technical Knowledge
We share:
-   **Service Manuals:** (Where available).
-   **OBD-II Codes:** What that error light actually means.
-   **Coding/Mods:** How to tweak the software (at your own risk).

### 3. Events
-   **"Frunk Meets":** Buy/Swap/Sell accessories in the car park.
-   **Range Challenges:** Real-world tests (e.g., "Can a 7X RWD actually make it to Albury on one charge?").
-   **Track Days:** For the 009 and X AWD owners who want to surprise some sports cars.
