# Public Charging in Australia: The Survival Guide

Charging an EV in Australia is different from the rest of the world. We have a mix of networks, plugs, and reliability issues. Here is how to survive.

## The Golden Rules

### 1. BYO Cable (The "Type 2")
**You cannot rely on finding a cable at every charger.**

-   **DC Fast Chargers (Highway):** Have tethered cables (built-in). You just plug in.
-   **AC Destination Chargers (Shopping Centers/Hotels):** Usually **DO NOT** have cables. They just have a socket on the wall.
-   **The Fix:** You *must* buy a **Type 2 to Type 2 (Mennekes)** cable. Keep it in the frunk or under the boot floor.
    -   *Cost:* ~$200-$300.
    -   *Length:* Get 7 meters. 5 meters is often too short if the charger is blocked.

### 2. Download These Apps (In Order)
Don't wait until you are at the charger to download the app and enter your credit card.

1.  **PlugShare:** The map of *all* chargers. Check recent check-ins. If the last 3 people said "Broken", believe them.
2.  **Chargefox:** The largest network. Most highway fast chargers are here.
3.  **Evie Networks:** The second largest. Very reliable, often at fast food outlets.
4.  **Tesla:** Download the Tesla app and create an account. Look for "Charge Your Non-Tesla". This opens up select Superchargers to your Zeekr 7X/X.
    -   *Note:* Park carefully. Tesla cables are short. You may need to park "incorrectly" to reach your port.

### 3. Understand Your Speed

| Charger Type | Speed | Time to Add 100km | Cable Needed? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 2 (AC)** | 7kW - 22kW | 1 - 3 Hours | **YES (BYO)** |
| **Fast (DC)** | 50kW | 20 Minutes | No |
| **Ultra-Rapid (DC)** | 350kW+ | 5 Minutes | No |

!!! info "Zeekr 7X Advantage"
    The Zeekr 7X is an **800V car**. When using a 350kW charger (like Chargefox Ultra-Rapid), you will get significantly faster speeds than a Tesla Model Y or BYD (which are 400V). Seek out the "350kW" units for maximum speed.

## Charging Etiquette (Don't be "That Guy")

-   **80% Rule:** On a busy highway charger, stop at 80%. The charging speed slows down massively after 80%. You are wasting everyone's time (and your money) to go to 100%.
-   **The "ICED" Spot:** If a petrol car is in a spot, check PlugShare. Sometimes the cable reaches the next bay.
-   **Check-in:** Use PlugShare to tell others "Charging successfully".

## Vic-Specific Tips

-   **RACV Solar:** Some RACV resorts have free or cheap charging for members. Check the app.
-   **Shopping Centers:** Highpoint, Chadstone, and Westfield Doncaster have large AC charging banks. Bring your Type 2 cable!
