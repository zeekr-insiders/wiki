# EV glossary

*Last checked: 8 November 2026.*

Plain definitions. Each term links to the page with the detail and the sources.

**AC (alternating current).** Mains power. Home and most destination charging is AC and the car's onboard charger converts it. [AC vs DC charging](charging-curve.md)

**BMS (battery management system).** The car's software and electronics that monitor and protect the pack, including limiting charge power as it fills. [Charging curve](charging-curve.md)

**CCS2 (Combined Charging System 2).** A Type 2 socket with two extra DC pins, the DC fast-charging plug used in Australia. [Plugs and cables](plugs-and-cables.md)

**CHAdeMO.** An older Japanese DC fast-charging standard, mostly on older imported cars. [Plugs and cables](plugs-and-cables.md)

**Charge limit.** The maximum state of charge you set the car to stop at. [Charging to 80% vs 100%](charging-80-vs-100.md)

**Charging curve.** A chart of charging power against state of charge. [Charging curve](charging-curve.md)

**DC (direct current).** What a battery stores. DC fast chargers supply it directly. [AC vs DC charging](charging-curve.md)

**DNSP (distribution network service provider).** The company that runs the local poles and wires, for example Ausgrid or Energex, and sets connection rules. [Home charging](home-charging.md)

**EVSE (electric vehicle supply equipment).** The proper name for a wallbox or charging post. [Home charging](home-charging.md)

**kW (kilowatt).** A rate of power. Charger speed and motor output. [kW vs kWh](kw-vs-kwh.md)

**kWh (kilowatt-hour).** An amount of energy. Battery size and your power bill. [kW vs kWh](kw-vs-kwh.md)

**LFP (lithium iron phosphate).** A tougher, longer-lasting battery chemistry with a flat voltage curve, used in Zeekr X RWD and 7X RWD. [NMC vs LFP](nmc-vs-lfp.md)

**LFP calibration.** A full charge that lets the car correct its estimate of how full an LFP battery is. [Charging to 80% vs 100%](charging-80-vs-100.md)

**NMC (nickel manganese cobalt).** A higher-energy-density battery chemistry used in the Zeekr X AWD, 7X Long Range and AWD, and 009. [NMC vs LFP](nmc-vs-lfp.md)

**Onboard charger.** The unit inside the car that converts AC to DC; its rating sets the AC charge speed. [AC vs DC charging](charging-curve.md)

**Phase (single-phase, three-phase).** How many live conductors a supply has. Single-phase tops out around 7 kW for a home charger, three-phase up to 22 kW. [Home charging](home-charging.md)

**Portable charger.** A cable with a built-in control box that plugs into a household socket; slow, and never used with an extension lead on a Zeekr. [Plugs and cables](plugs-and-cables.md)

**RCD (residual current device).** A safety switch that cuts power on a fault. [Plugs and cables](plugs-and-cables.md)

**SOC (state of charge).** The battery percentage. [Charging curve](charging-curve.md)

**Tethered / untethered.** Whether the cable is attached to the charger or you bring your own. [Plugs and cables](plugs-and-cables.md)

**Type 2.** The standard AC plug in Australia. [Plugs and cables](plugs-and-cables.md)

**V2G (vehicle to grid).** Exporting energy from the car to the electricity network. [V2L, V2H and V2G](v2l-v2h-v2g.md)

**V2H (vehicle to home).** Powering a house from the car, for example during an outage. [V2L, V2H and V2G](v2l-v2h-v2g.md)

**V2L (vehicle to load).** Powering appliances directly from the car; 3.3 kW on the 7X. [V2L, V2H and V2G](v2l-v2h-v2g.md)

**WLTP.** The laboratory test cycle behind Zeekr's published range figures. [Range in the real world](real-world-range.md)

**800V.** A higher-voltage electrical architecture that allows higher charging power with lower current. [Charging curve](charging-curve.md)

!!! note "Sourcing"
    Each definition restates the page it links to, and the footnotes are on that page. "Onboard charger", "BMS", "EVSE", "RCD" and "DNSP" are standard terms; where a linked page has no source for one, it says so.
